# One message of the user as a `request` of the store: `ap request`

Purpose: every agent the user speaks to records the user's message as a `request` of the store in the turn it arrives, in the user's own words read from the session's record where the verb can read it, routes it to an intent or sets it aside, and lists the current requests of a topic before it asks the user anything. Where the CLI runs a hook when the user submits a prompt (Claude Code's `UserPromptSubmit`), the verb itself is that hook: it records the prompt with its words read from the session record before the agent's first tool call and tells the agent the request's key, and the hook that runs when a turn ends (`Stop`) tells the agent once that requests of the session are unrouted. A CLI without such hooks keeps the agent's `ap request`.

## Requirements

- R1: The arguments are `--store <absolute path>`, forwarded to every `ont` call, and `--text`, admitted only with `op` `list`; any other argument, and `--text` with another `op`, is refused `usage` (field `args`) before any call to the store.
- R2: The request is one JSON object of at most 1 MiB with `op` `record`, `supersede`, `route` or `list`, or, as the fifth form, a hook's input (R26); an unknown, missing or malformed member is refused `invalid_input` with its field before any call to the store. A `route` that has none or more than one of its four forms (the member `route` of `record` and `supersede`, and the `op` `route`) is refused with the field `route`.
- R3: `request_id` is 1 to 50 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`; `agent` and `session` are 1 to 64 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:` and `-`; `topic` is 1 to 120 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:`, `/` and `-`; `kind` is one of the schema's `request_kind`; `reading` is 1 to 1,000 bytes; `runtime` is 1 to 40 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`.
- R4: A refusal is one line `{"error":{"code","field","detail","after","request"}}` on standard error with exit 2: `after` is null when this call applied nothing, `record` when the call recorded the request (also `already_applied`), or `supersede` for `route_unknown` (R24), and with `record` or `supersede` `request` is that request's native key, else null. `after` null with `process_failed` or `store_answer_unreadable` does not show that nothing was applied: the call may have been cut after an apply the verb did not see answered. The caller repeats the same call with the same `request_id`, never a new one: what was applied answers `already_applied`, and the rest is applied.
- R5: A refusal of the store keeps the store's code (the first failed criterion's code for `submission_criteria`) and has the store's refusal object, as the store answered it, as `detail`.
- R6: The actions use the request ids `<request_id>-record`, `<request_id>-supersede` and `<request_id>-route`, the client `ap`, and the request's `agent` and `session` as the actor, so a repeated call is the same request only with the same `agent` and `session`.
- R7: `words_from` takes the string of the line of the session record whose `timestamp` begins with its `at` and that is a line of `type` `user` with a `message.content` that is a string, or of `type` `queue-operation` with `operation` `enqueue` and a string `content` (a message sent while a turn ran); a line whose `isMeta`, `isCompactSummary` or `isSidechain` is true is no line of the user, and byte-equal strings count as one; no such line is `words_not_found` and more than one distinct string `words_ambiguous`, each with the count as `detail`.
- R8: Words read from a record are stored with `words_verified` true, `record` the record's path and `said_at` the line's own time to the second with `Z`.
- R9: Typed words are `words`, or `words_file` (an absolute path of a regular file whose whole content is the words, at most 1 MiB of the bytes the host's `read` returns, else `input_too_large` with field `words_file`; not valid UTF-8 is `words_not_utf8`); the record of `words_from` is read whole, whatever its size, with the required `said_at` (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`), and are stored with `words_verified` false.
- R10: The words are never trimmed, normalised or changed, and empty words are refused `words_empty` before any call to the store.
- R11: Words over 24,000 bytes, or whose JSON string form without its two quotes is over 48,000 bytes, are stored as their first 4,000 bytes cut back to a character boundary with `truncated` true; words within the bound are stored whole with `truncated` false.
- R12: A `route` is exactly one of `{"intent": key}`, `{"aside": reason}` (1 to 600 bytes), `{"unit": key}` and `{"finding": key}`; an intent and a reason are checked before anything is applied, the intent with one `ont get`: an intent that does not exist is refused `unknown_intent`, one that is `met` or `retired` `intent_closed`, both with `after` null.
- R13: `record` applies `record_request` without a `validate` before it, then reads the created request back and compares its `words` byte for byte with the words sent (`readback_differs`, `after` `record`), and only then applies the route. The read-back reads the request's head, not its observations (`ont get` answers every observation of an object, up to 100, which passes 1 MiB for a request routed often, though an answer of `ont` is read up to 8 MiB): it takes, from `ont search` on `request` filtered by the call's `topic` and `said_at` and followed to the end (not by `session`: it is optional, `required: false` in `ontology/work.schema.json`, and on this engine a filter on an optional property answers `qualified: false` once one scanned object lacks it, `ontology/README.md`, Version 8), the row whose id is the request's key; no such row is `store_answer_unreadable` (field `request`).
- R14: A refused route leaves the request recorded and unrouted with `after` `record`; the same call with a corrected `route` answers `already_applied` for the record and applies the route; a call repeated whole answers `already_applied` for each action and writes nothing.
- R15: The answer of `record` is one JSON object on standard output with `request`, `record` (`applied` or `already_applied`), `route` (`applied`, `already_applied` or null), `words_bytes` (of the stored words), `words_verified` and `truncated`.
- R16: `supersede` applies `supersede_request` on the current request `request`, reads the new request back as `record` does, then applies the `route` given or, without one, the route the replaced request had (read after the supersede, from its head: its subject, whatever its type, when its route is `subject`; aside with its reason; its intent, unit or finding, with the reference it holds, as a subject, when it was routed before schema version 24 (route `intent`, `unit` or `finding`); an unrouted one stays unrouted; the row whose id is the replaced key among those `ont search` on `request` filtered by `status` `superseded` gives, followed to the end, and no such row is `store_answer_unreadable`, field `supersedes`), and answers as `record` with `supersedes`.
- R17: The route inherited by `supersede` is not checked before the supersede. When its subject is an intent, it gets the check of R12 (one `ont get`) after the supersede and before `route_request` is applied: `unknown_intent` or `intent_closed` has `after` `record`, and the same call with a `route` given finishes it. A store refusal of the route has `after` `record` too.
- R18: `route` and the member `route` apply `route_request` (`intent`, `unit` or `finding`, with `subject` the object of that type and that key) or `set_request_aside` (`aside`) on the request with `<request_id>-route`, after the check of R12; the store's refusal of a unit or a finding that does not exist (`unknown_subject`) is passed on; `route` answers `request` and `route`.
- R19: `list` reads with `ont search` on the type `request` (`status` `current` and the `topic`, or without a topic `status` `current` and `route` `unrouted`), follows `continuation` until there is none, requires `basis.qualified` (else `store_answer_unqualified`), reads no view and writes nothing.
- R20: `list` answers `{"requests":[...]}` with the topic's current requests newest first, or the unrouted ones oldest first, sorted by `said_at` and then by key, each with the words and the reading as the store holds them and with the key of the intent, unit, finding or host it is routed to: for a stored route `subject`, `route` is the subject's type and its key is in the member of that name (`intent`, `unit`, `finding` or `host`); a request of a stored route `intent`, `unit` or `finding` (written before schema version 24) is listed as it is stored; `host` is null otherwise.
- R21: `list --text` prints one block per request: `<said_at> <kind> <topic>`, ` [not verified]` and ` [truncated]` when they apply and the route (` -> unrouted`, ` -> intent <key>`, ` -> unit <key>`, ` -> finding <key>`, ` -> host <key>`, ` -> aside (<reason>)`) on the first line, then the reading, then the words as they are.
- R22: Every call to `ont` goes through the module `store.rs`, which retries an answer `store_busy` up to five times after a short wait and retries nothing else.
- R23: The verb writes nothing but through the actions `record_request`, `supersede_request`, `route_request` and `set_request_aside`, and writes no file. The hook form (R26) writes only through `record_request` and writes no file.
- R24: A `route` of the replaced request that the verb does not know is never read as unrouted: `supersede` without `route` is refused `route_unknown` (field `supersedes`, `detail` the value) with `after` `supersede` and the new request's key, and the same call with a `route` given finishes it.
- R25: `words_from.record` (of `record`, of `supersede` and of the hook form) is admitted only when the string is `<home>/.claude/projects/` followed by one or more components, none of them empty, `.` or `..`, with no control character, at most 400 bytes. `<home>` is the home of the passwd entry of the user the verb runs as (its effective user id), without a final `/`; the variables `HOME` and `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` are not read. Any other value, and every value when no passwd entry names that user, is refused `invalid_input`, field `words_from.record`, `after` null, before any call to the store, whether or not the file exists and whatever it holds. The string is compared: no symbolic link is resolved. R7, R8 and R9 keep their rule.
- R26: A request that is one JSON object with a string member `hook_event_name` and no member `op` is a hook's input. The arguments stay those of R1: `--store` is forwarded, and `--text` or any argument R1 refuses is `usage` (R4) with a hook's input too. Of the input only `hook_event_name`, `session_id`, `transcript_path`, `cwd`, `prompt` and `stop_hook_active` are read; every other member is ignored. `UserPromptSubmit` is handled by R27, `Stop` by R28; any other value prints nothing, reads no file and calls no store. An object with both `op` and `hook_event_name` is no hook's input: its `op` refuses the member (R2).
- R27: The input of `UserPromptSubmit`, in this order: (a) `session_id` has the form of `session` (R3), `transcript_path` is admitted by R25 and `prompt` is a nonempty string, else the end is `not recorded` with `invalid_input`; (b) the record is read whole, and the message's line is the last line of `type` `user` whose `message.content` is a string, with none of `isMeta`, `isCompactSummary` and `isSidechain` true, whose `origin.kind` is the string `human` and whose string equals `prompt` byte for byte (a `queue-operation` line never is); it is admitted only when no line of `type` `assistant` but one whose `isSidechain` is true follows it; a record that is absent or unreadable, holds no such line or holds one an answer follows ends `not recorded` with `words_not_found`, nothing applied (the first prompt of a session, whose record does not exist yet when the hook runs, ends so); (c) `at` is the first 19 bytes of that line's `timestamp`, of the form `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS`, else `words_not_found`, and the words R7 reads at `at` are exactly one string, equal to `prompt`, else `not recorded` with R7's code; (d) the message is recorded as `record` with `words_from` `{record, at}` does (R8, R10, R11, the read-back of R13), with no route, `request_id` `hook-` and the first 40 hex digits of the SHA-256 of `session_id`, a line feed and `at`, `agent` `hook`, `session` the `session_id`, `runtime` `claude`, `kind` `order`, `reading` `Recorded by the hook of the CLI; kind and reading are not yet an agent's.`, and `topic` the first component of `cwd` below the work root when `cwd` lies under it, else the last component of `cwd`, each byte outside the set of R3 written as `-`, cut to 120 bytes, `hook` when that is empty or `cwd` is no string; (e) the end is `recorded` only when the apply answered `applied` and the read-back passed; `already_applied` ends `not recorded` with `words_not_found` and no read-back follows (a line that has its request is an earlier message's with the same text), and a refusal of the store or a failed call ends `not recorded` with the code R4 would name; (f) exactly one line is printed on standard output: `ap request: recorded <key> (verified, unrouted) by the hook. Do not record this message again: route it (op route, request <key>), or supersede it with your kind and reading and "words_from":{"record":"<transcript_path>","at":"<at>"}.` (the path written as in a JSON string), or `ap request: not recorded by the hook (<code>). If this message is the user's, record it with ap request; its session record is <transcript_path>.`, the last clause (from `;`) left out when `transcript_path` is not admitted.
- R28: The input of `Stop`: when `stop_hook_active` is `true`, nothing is printed and the store is not called. Else one search as `list` without a topic makes it (R19, every page, qualified), and of its rows those whose `session` equals `session_id`, oldest first as R20 orders them. None: nothing is printed. One or more: exactly one line on standard output, the JSON object `{"decision":"block","reason":"ap request: <n> unrouted request(s) of this session: <keys>. Route each (op route) or set it aside, then end the turn."}`, `<keys>` the first five keys joined by `, `. A refusal, an unqualified answer or a failed call prints nothing. This form reads no file and applies no action.
- R29: Once the input is a hook's input (R26) and the arguments are none or `--store <path>`, the status is 0 in every case of R26 to R28 (4 only when standard output cannot be written), nothing is written on standard error and no refusal of R4 is printed. A refusal of R1 (`usage`) keeps exit 2 and its line on standard error with a hook's input too, as does an input over 1 MiB or one that is no JSON, which are refused before the form is known.

## Interface

`ap request [--store <path>] [--text] < request.json`. The request is one JSON object with `op`, or a hook's input (R26):

- `record`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session` (also stored as the request's `session`), optional `runtime`, `topic`, `kind`, `reading`, exactly one of `words_from` (`{"record": <path of a Claude Code session record under <home>/.claude/projects/ of the user the verb runs as (R25), at most 400 bytes>, "at": "YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS"}`), `words` and `words_file` (both with `said_at`), and optional `route`.
- `supersede`: `request` (the native key of the current request it replaces) and the members of `record`.
- `route`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `request`, and exactly one of `intent` (an intent's key), `aside` (a reason), `unit` (a unit's key) and `finding` (a finding's native key); the member `route` of `record` and `supersede` takes the same four forms. `route_request` with a subject needs version 24 of the schema.
- `list`: optional `topic`.
- A hook's input: the object Claude Code gives its command hook on standard input, with `hook_event_name` `UserPromptSubmit` (`session_id`, `transcript_path`, `cwd`, `prompt`) or `Stop` (`session_id`, `stop_hook_active`); other events and other members are ignored. Its answer is at most one line on standard output with status 0 (R27 to R29); the registration of the hook is `content/runtime-references/CLI-RUNTIMES.md` section 6.

The answer of `record` and `supersede`: `{"request","record","route","words_bytes","words_verified","truncated"}`, with `supersedes` (the replaced request's key) for `supersede`. The answer of `route`: `{"request","route"}`.

The answer of `list`: `{"requests":[{"request","topic","kind","said_at","words_verified","truncated","route","intent","unit","finding","host","route_reason","reading","words"}]}`, where `intent`, `unit`, `finding` and `host` are the key the request is routed to and `route_reason` is the reason while the route is `aside`, each null otherwise.

Codes of `ap` itself: `usage`, `invalid_input`, `input_too_large`, `words_not_found`, `words_ambiguous`, `words_not_utf8`, `words_empty`, `unknown_intent`, `intent_closed`, `route_unknown`, `readback_differs`, `store_answer_unreadable`, `store_answer_unqualified`, `process_failed`.

A refusal of the store passes on the store's code, such as `said_in_the_future`, `request_not_current`, `request_confirms_an_intent`, `intent_closed`, `unknown_subject`, `invalid_parameter`, `request_id_reused` or `store_busy`.

## Not included

- No reader of an Antigravity session record and no hook for it: its messages are recorded as typed words, not verified.
- No session record outside `<home>/.claude/projects/` gives verified words, and no symbolic link under it is resolved.
- No record by the hook of a message sent while a turn runs (a `queue-operation` line), of a prompt the record does not hold as a human's line when the hook runs, or of a slash command: these stay the agent's `ap request`. The hook does not wait for the record, does not try again and keeps no state between two calls; it bounds no age of the message's line.
- No write of a CLI's settings file: the hook's registration is a change of the host's configuration made by hand.
- No verb for intents or criteria, and no `ap status`.
- No view is read: a view's cells are escaped and bounded, and `list` shows the words as they are.
