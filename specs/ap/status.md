# What is asked, planned, running, waiting and broken, and what a word means: `ap status`

Purpose: every agent starts with one read of the store instead of a state file: what the user asked and has not seen routed, the intents with their criteria, the units, the running stages, the open findings, the holds and the last delivery, what the Lead acts on first, one intent or unit in full, what a word of the life cycle means (`--term`), when a unit of an intent needs the Lead (`--wait`), and how long, how often repaired and how often stopped the merged units took (`--indicators`), and an intent in at most forty short lines (`--brief`); and the verb writes nothing.

## Requirements

- R1: The arguments are `--store <absolute path>`, `--text`, `--term <word>`, one key, `--requests` (with a key only, R42), the form of R23, the form of R34, the form of R43 and the form of R47, each at most once; `--term` and a key together, a key that is empty, over 512 bytes, begins with `-` or holds a control character, and a word that is not 1 to 80 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `_`, `-` and `.` are refused `usage` (field `args`, exit 2) before any call to the store. The verb reads no standard input.
- R2: The verb calls the store only with `get`, `search`, `render`, `describe`, `aggregate`, `log` (`log` by `--indicators` and `--page` only) and `fn` (by `--page` only, R48), applies no action and writes no file, whatever its arguments (the wait of R23 to R33 also reads a policy file, a units directory and the service manager, and still writes nothing).
- R3: Every list is read with `ont search` filtered by required properties only, paged to the end; a page that is not `basis.qualified` is refused `store_answer_unqualified` with the list's name as `field`; rows are then filtered and sorted by the verb.
- R4: Without a key the answer is one JSON object with `at`, `schema`, `needs_lead`, `requests_unrouted`, `intents`, `units`, `runs`, `findings`, `holds`, `delivery`; `at` is the member `now` of the envelope of the verb's first answer of the store (seconds since 1970) as UTC to the second, `schema` is that envelope's `schema.work.version`; no value the verb prints is typed into it.
- R5: `requests_unrouted` is `{count, oldest}`: the number of current requests with route `unrouted` and the oldest 20 by `said_at`, then key, each `{request, topic, kind, said_at, words_verified}`.
- R6: `intents` holds every intent whose status is `draft`, `assumed` or `confirmed`, by key, each `{intent, status, confirmed, lead_session, outcome, outcome_cut, criteria, units}`: `confirmed` as `ap intent show` gives it; `outcome` its first 200 bytes cut back to a character boundary with `outcome_cut` true when cut; `criteria` the counts `open`, `pass`, `fail`, `waived` of its criteria; `units` the count of its units by state.
- R7: `units` holds every unit in the state `ready`, `running`, `accepted` or `stopped`, ordered `stopped`, `accepted`, `running`, `ready`, then by key, each `{unit, intent, lane, class, wave, state, repository, pull_request, head, depends_on}` (`wave` the unit's, null for a unit without one) with `waits_on` only for `ready` and `stop_cause` only for `stopped`; `intent` and `depends_on` are native keys.
- R8: `runs` holds every run with status `running`, oldest `started_at` first, each `{run, unit, intent, role, runtime, model, started_at}`, `unit` and `intent` null where absent.
- R9: `findings` holds every finding `open` or `planned`: `urgent` first, then `act`, then `info`, newest `last_seen_at` first within each, each `{finding, severity, kind, state, summary, last_seen_at}`.
- R10: `holds` holds every `active` hold, oldest `set_at` first, each `{hold, effect, until, subject, unit, intent, scope}`, null where absent: `subject` is `{type, key}` of the hold's `subject`, `unit` and `intent` its key when the subject is of that type. A hold written before schema version 24 has no `subject` and reads null in all three. Every reading of what a hold names (R14's `here`, R15, R30, R46 and the text forms) reads `subject`; a subject of another type than unit or intent is printed as ` <type> <key>` where a unit is printed.
- R11: `delivery` is `{at, end, tip, target}` of the record `<state>/last.json`, where `<state>` is the member `state` of `/etc/agent-platform/deliver.json` (an absolute path; `/var/lib/agent-platform/deliver` when that file cannot be read or has none), both read through the host's `read`; when the record cannot be read `delivery` is null and `delivery_unreadable` is true.
- R12: `needs_lead` is an array of `{kind, key, why}` in this order: findings `urgent` and `open` (`why` the summary); units `stopped` (the stop cause); units `accepted` (waits for the Lead's reading or merge); findings `act` and `open`; requests `unrouted` whose `said_at` is more than 86,400 seconds before `at`.
- R13: With a key, the key is looked up as an intent, then as a unit; neither is `unknown_key` (field `key`); a key that names both prints the intent with `also_unit` true.
- R14: An intent is printed as `ap intent show` prints it (every property, `criteria`, `requests` as R42 bounds them, `requests_count`, `confirmed`), but with its criteria by `added_at`, then key (`ap intent show` orders one draft's criteria by the log), then `units` (all its units, every state: stopped, accepted, running, ready, draft, merged, done, dropped, then by key, the members of R7 and `ended_at`, `merged_commit`), `findings` (those planned into it or into one of its units, and every open one, the members of R9), `plan_reviews` (its reviews of kind `plan`, newest `started_at` first, each `{review, head, round, status, verdict, started_at, closed_at}`) and `holds` (every active hold, the members of R10 and `here`: those that name the intent or one of its units first with `here` true).
- R15: A unit is printed with every property it holds, `unit` its key, `reviews` (each `{review, kind, head, round, status, verdict, closed_at, report}`), `runs` (each `{run, role, model, status, state, started_at, ended_at, tokens}`, `tokens` `{input, cache_read, cache_write, output}`), `holds` (the active holds that name it) and `blocked_by` (each unit of `depends_on` that is not `merged` or `done`, `{unit, state}`).
- R16: The reviews and runs of a unit are found through the views `reviews_of_unit` and `runs_of_unit` (the Key column), each read with `get`: a view has no report and no token column and its cells are escaped; a view answer with `truncated` true is `store_answer_unqualified`.
- R17: `--text` without a key prints at most 80 plain lines: `status <at> schema work@<n>`, then the sections `NEEDS THE LEAD`, `INTENTS`, `UNITS`, `RUNNING`, `FINDINGS`, `HOLDS`, `REQUESTS NOT ROUTED`, `DELIVERY` in this order, each with its heading and at most 9 entries and, when cut, `(+N more)`; a section without entries prints `none`; a line break or control character inside a stored value is a space (R18's form with a key writes `?` instead). A unit's line of `UNITS` has ` w<wave>` directly after its class for a unit that has a wave, and is as before for one without.
- R18: `--text` with a key prints every member as a labelled line, or for a text with a line break as the label and the text's lines each after two spaces, with no bound; a control character other than the line break is `?` (in this form only: R17 and R21 write a control character as a space).
- R19: `--term <word>` reads `ont describe` once and matches the word, in lower case, against the names of the types, actions, properties, parameters, values of enumerated properties, links, views and criteria (their `code`) of the schema, and against the name of a value type, which matches the properties and parameters typed by it; the matches are `{kind, name, of, text}`, exact names first, then names that contain the word, within each by kind in the order above, then by `of`, then by name, at most 12, with `more` the count beyond, so that `--term unit` begins with the type; no match is an empty `matches`, exit 0.
- R20: The members of a value type come from at most one `ont aggregate` per distinct value type, asked on a property typed by it (a second property of the type only after a `derive_forbidden` of the first one's object type), `{"object_type":T,"group_by":[P],"metrics":["count"],"where":{"in":[P,[]]}}`; the keys of the groups are the members, each matching as kind `value`, once for every property typed by that value type, with `of` `<type>.<property>` and the property's description as `text`; an answer without groups or a refusal (such as `derive_forbidden`) means no members and is skipped, never an error of the verb.
- R21: A link's `text` is `<from>.<via> -> <to>`, a view's its type and arguments, a criterion's its message; `--term --text` prints one line per match `<kind> <of>.<name>: <text>` (`<kind> <name>: <text>` without `of`) and `(+N more)` when cut; a control character in the text is a space and the description is never printed whole; a match whose text is empty (a property without a description, and its values) prints `(no description)` as its text, while the JSON `text` stays `""`.
- R22: A refusal is one line `{"error":{"code","field","detail"}}` on standard error with exit 2, the code of a refusal of the store being the store's own; every call to `ont` goes through the module `store.rs`.
- R23: `ap status --wait <intent> [--timeout <seconds>] [--store <path>]`: `--timeout` is an integer of digits from 30 to 86,400 (default 14,400); the intent is a key as R1 shapes one. `--wait` with `--text`, `--term`, a key, a second `--wait` or `--timeout`, a `--timeout` without `--wait`, and a timeout not of that form are `usage` (field `args`, exit 2) before any call. An intent the store does not have is `unknown_intent` (field `intent`, exit 2) at once, at any reading.
- R24: The wait applies no action, writes no file or directory and takes no `work.lock`: it reads the store (R2's calls, one `get` of the intent, the searches of R3 for units, findings and holds, and one `get` of each `ready` or `accepted` unit that has a repository and a key of `ap unit add`'s form, to read its revision), the policy file, the units directory (`<key>/lock` by the shared attempt of `ap work`, `<key>/started`) and `systemctl list-units`, and starts nothing.
- R25: The first reading is of the units of the intent (every state) and the open findings, as `ap status <intent>` reads them; then a reading every 30 seconds (the trait's `pause`) until a reason of R26 to R30 holds or the timeout passes. The answer is one JSON object `{reason, unit, finding, at}` and a line break, exit 0: `unit` and `finding` the key or null, `at` the member `now` of the envelope of the first answer of the store in the reading that gave the reason (of the last reading that succeeded for `timeout`) as UTC to the second, as R4 writes it.
- R26: `unit_accepted`: a unit of the intent of class `contract` or `effect` that waits for the Lead's `approve` (`approved_head` not equal to `head`) is `accepted` with another `head` than at the first reading, or was not `accepted` then. A `plain` unit, and one whose head is approved, gives none.
- R27: `unit_stopped`: a unit of the intent is `stopped` with another `ended_at` than at the first reading, or was not `stopped` then.
- R28: `finding`: the open findings (state `open`) of severity `act` or `urgent`, of any intent, hold a key the first reading did not hold.
- R29: `all_ended`: every unit of the intent is `merged`, `done` or `dropped`; an intent with no unit ends so at once.
- R30: `unit_idle`: a unit of the intent that has a repository and a key of `ap unit add`'s form is not in flight (`ap work`'s reading: a live run unit `<prefix>-<key>.service`, or the unit's lock held) and is `running` (a cut run), or `ready` or `accepted` with a file `started` equal to its revision, and no active hold has the unit or its intent as its subject, at two successive readings that succeeded (a failed reading between them does not break "successive"). It is read by `observe` of `crates/ap/src/work.rs` under the units directory and the prefix of the policy file as `ap work` reads them. A unit whose run lives (a merge run of an `accepted` unit included) gives none.
- R31: `unit_idle` is never given where the host has no service manager, where there is no policy file, where the policy has no member `work`, where exactly one of `--store` and a non-empty `AP_POLICY_FILE` is present (the policy file is then not read), or where `--store` is given and the policy is one `ap work` refuses with `--store` (`units.directory`, `work.slice` or `work.prefix` missing): a scratch store is never read against the host's units directory.
- R32: `timeout`: the timeout in seconds, counted by the trait's `now` from the first reading, has passed after a reading; `unit` and `finding` are null. When two reasons hold at one reading the first of R26 to R30 is given (R26, R27, R28, R29, R30), and among units the smallest key.
- R33: A reading that fails (a store call, `systemctl`, a policy that `work::policy`, `work::work` or `drive::units` refuses `policy_invalid`) is no reason: the wait goes on at the next reading and compares with the first reading that succeeded; the third failed reading in a row ends it with that error, and a timeout with no reading that succeeded ends it with the last error, each exit 3.

- R34: `ap status --indicators [<intent>] [--text] [--store <path>]`: the intent is a key as R1 shapes one. `--indicators` with `--term`, `--wait`, `--timeout`, a second key or a second `--indicators` is `usage` (field `args`, exit 2) before any call. An intent the store does not have is `unknown_intent` (field `intent`, exit 2) after one `get` of it. The form applies no action, writes no file and reads no file.
- R35: The answer is one JSON object `{at, units, summary}` and a line break; `at` as R4 gives it (the `now` of the envelope of the first answer of the store). `units` holds one row for each unit in the state `merged` (those of the intent when one is named, else every one), ordered by key, each `{unit, intent, class, implementer, started_at, merged_at, minutes, rounds, repairs, first_verdict, stops, tokens}` and, only when R39 says so, `log_cut` true: `intent` the native key or null, `implementer` `{runtime, model, effort}` of the unit's page or null. The units are one `ont search` by `state` (R3); with none, the log is not read.
- R36: `started_at` is the `submitted_at` of the unit's first `start_unit` row of the log, `merged_at` that of its `merge_unit` row, `minutes` the time between them rounded down to the minute (null where either is null or the merge is earlier than the start), `stops` the `cause` of each of its `stop_unit` rows in the order they were applied. A `start_unit` row after the first (a unit started again) is not read.
- R37: `rounds` is the number of the unit's reviews of kind `packet` whose status is `closed` and whose verdict is `accept` or `repair` (an `abandoned` verdict, a review `running` or `revoked`, and a review of another kind are none), a review with `carried_from` left out of that count alone (one `carry_review` made, `specs/ap/unit-run.md` R37: a verdict but not a round); `first_verdict` the verdict of the first of them by `closed_at`, then key (null with none); `repairs` the number of its runs of role `fixer`; `tokens` an object by role of its runs, each `{input, cache_read, cache_write, output}` the sum of the run objects' `input_tokens`, `cache_read_tokens`, `cache_write_tokens`, `output_tokens` over the role's runs that hold the member, null where none of them does. The reviews and runs are read as R16 reads those of a unit.
- R38: The log is read with `ont log` for each of `start_unit`, `merge_unit` and `stop_unit` once, as `{"action_type", "outcome": "applied", "parameters": true, "limit": 100}`, followed by `continuation` to the end (a repeated continuation is `store_answer_unreadable`, field `log`, as is an answer without `rows` or `withheld`); the verb keeps the rows whose `parameters.unit.native_key` is the unit's and reverses them (the log answers newest first) before "first" and "in order" are read. The reading is one public function of `store.rs`.
- R39: A merged unit with no `start_unit` row or no `merge_unit` row has those members null and `log_cut` true. A `withheld` above 0 in the answer for an action type makes that action's members null (`started_at` and `minutes`; `merged_at` and `minutes`; `stops`) and `log_cut` true on every row. No row of `stop_unit` is no stop (`stops` empty), not a cut.
- R40: `summary` is `{count, median_minutes, median_rounds, median_repairs, first_accept_share}`: the count of rows; the median of `minutes`, of `rounds` and of `repairs` over the rows where the member is not null (the mean of the two middle values for an even count, null with no value); the share of the rows with a `first_verdict` whose `first_verdict` is `accept`, rounded to three places (null when no row has one). No member counts the updates of `ap unit run`: every call applies as one actor and the log does not tell one update from another.
- R41: `--text` prints one line for each row, in the order of R35, then one line of the summary: `<unit> <intent> <class> <runtime>/<model>/<effort> <started_at> <merged_at> <minutes> <rounds> <repairs> <first_verdict> <stops> <tokens>` and ` log_cut` when cut; a null member is `-`, `stops` are the causes joined by `,` (`none` when empty, `-` when null), `tokens` are `<role>:<input>/<cache_read>/<cache_write>/<output>` joined by `,` by role (`-` with no run); then `summary <count> units median minutes <m> rounds <r> repairs <p> first accepted <share>`. A control character in a stored value is a space.

- R42: `requests` of an intent in R14, as JSON and as `--text`, holds the newest five of the requests routed to it (still the rows of `ap intent show`, oldest first by `said_at`), and `requests_count` the number of all of them; with `--requests` (allowed only with a key, with or without `--text`; for a key that names only a unit it changes nothing and that answer has neither member) `requests` holds every one. The cut is made by `ap status`: the answer of `ap intent show` does not change.
- R43: `ap status --brief <intent> [--store <path>]`: the intent is a key as R1 shapes one. `--brief` takes only `--store`: with `--text`, `--requests`, a key, `--term`, `--wait`, `--timeout`, `--indicators` or a second `--brief` it is `usage` (field `args`, exit 2) before any call. A key that is not an intent is `unknown_intent` (field `intent`, exit 2). The answer is plain text, not JSON, and the form makes only the calls of R2, reads only the files of R11 and writes nothing.
- R44: The brief is at most 40 lines, each at most 120 bytes with its line break (a longer line is cut at a character boundary); it has no heading line, each line begins with the label of its section (`status`, `criterion`, `unit`, `needs`, `hold`, `delivery`); a line break or control character inside a stored value is a space.
- R45: The sections come in this order, each with its share of lines: `status` (2: the intent, its status and who confirmed it, then its Lead's session, `-` when it has none); `criterion` (8: the key and the state of each criterion, by `added_at` then key); `unit` (16, and every line the other sections do not use: each unit of the intent that is not `merged`, `done` or `dropped`, in the order of R14, with its state and class (and directly after the class ` w<wave>` for a unit that has a wave) and, for a `ready` unit its `waits_on`, for a `stopped` unit its cause, for another unit its dependencies that are not `merged` or `done` as `blocked_by` of R15 counts them with their states); `needs` (6); `hold` (5); `delivery` (1). A section with more entries than its share shows its share less one and a last line `<label> (+N more)`, N the entries not shown.
- R46: `needs` holds what R12 orders, limited to the intent: the findings (state `open`) that name the intent or one of its units, or name neither a unit nor an intent; the intent's `stopped` and `accepted` units; the requests `unrouted` for more than 86,400 seconds that name neither a unit nor an intent; as `needs <kind> <key>: <why>`. `hold` holds the active holds that name the intent or one of its units (`here` true in R14), then those that name neither a unit nor an intent, as `hold <effect> until <until>` with the unit, the intent and the scope the hold names. `delivery` is the record of R11 as `delivery <at> <end> tip <tip> target <target>`, or `delivery unreadable`.

- R47: `ap status --page [--store <path>]`: the status page, the text the service `ap-status-page.service` writes. `--page` takes only `--store`: with `--text`, a key, `--requests`, `--term`, `--wait`, `--timeout`, `--indicators`, `--brief` or a second `--page` it is `usage` (field `args`, exit 2) before any call. The answer is plain lines, not JSON.
- R48: The calls of `--page` are those of R2 and, through `store.rs`, `ont fn` of `cost` (once without an intent and once for each intent of R6), `capacity` and `api_spend` (once each), and no other function; the rows of R35 to R39 are read once, whatever the number of intents; only the files of R11 are read; no action is applied and nothing is written. The calls are made in this order, whatever the order the lines print in (R49): the reads of R4 and R17; `capacity`; `api_spend`; the brief of each intent of R6, by key; the rows of R35 to R39; `cost` without an intent; `cost` for each intent of R6, by key. No step after the reads of R4 begins once 600 seconds have passed since the form's first call of the store, by the port's clock (`now`); each section of a step not begun is `unreadable page_bound` (R53). So `capacity` and `api_spend` are always asked, and as merged units grow the `cost` sections asked last are the ones cut.
- R49: The page's lines come in this order: the lines `ap status --text` prints (R17) for the same answers; then `WEEK <since> <until>`, `until` the UTC day of `at` (R4) and `since` six days before it, both `YYYY-MM-DD`; then R50 for each intent of R6, by key; then R51; then R52.
- R50: An intent: `INTENT <intent>`; the lines `ap status --brief <intent>` prints (R44 to R46); `flow <intent> <count> merged, <week> in the week, median minutes <m> rounds <r> repairs <p> first accepted <share>`, over the rows of R35 whose `intent` is the intent: `<count>` to `<share>` their summary as R40 computes it and R41 writes it, `<week>` the rows whose `merged_at` day is from `since` to `until`, and ` log_cut` at the end when a row has it; one line `cost <intent> <key> <usd> USD <runs> runs` for each of the `groups` of `cost` asked `{"since","until","by":"role","intent"}`, in the answer's order; then `cost <intent> total <total_usd> USD, merged <merged>, a pr <usd_a_pr>, lead share <lead_share>`.
- R51: All: `ALL`; the flow line of R50 over every row of R35, with `all` for the intent; the cost lines of R50 for `cost` asked `{"since","until","by":"role"}`, with `all` for the intent and ` unmeasured <unmeasured>` at the end of the total line; for each of the `jobs` of `api_spend` asked `{"month"}` (the `YYYY-MM` of `until`): `api <month> <job> settled <s> reserved <r> counted <c> USD, <runs> runs, <running> running`; then `api <month> total settled <s> reserved <r> counted <c> USD`; each amount its `*_usd_micros` in USD to the cent, in integer arithmetic, rounded half away from zero (12,345,000 is `12.35`, 5,000 is `0.01`, -5,000 is `-0.01`).
- R52: Capacity, of `capacity` asked `{}`: `HOSTS`; for each of `hosts`: `host <host> machine <machine> node <node> answers <answers> stale <stale> free <free> cpus <cpus> load <l> available <available_mb> MB seen <seen_at>`, `<l>` its `load_x100` written exactly as `<load_x100 / 100>.<load_x100 % 100 in two digits>` (5 is `0.05`); for each of `machines`: `machine <machine> up <up> free <free> cpus <cpus> load <l> available <available_mb> MB`; `can start <the machines of can_start joined by ,>` (`none` when empty); `ACCOUNTS`; for each of `accounts`: `account <account> <runtime> allowance <allowance> source <source> at <at>`, an allowance that is not a string written as compact JSON.
- R53: In R50 to R52 a null is `-`; a line break or control character inside a value is a space (R17's rule); a number that R51 or R52 does not convert is written as the answer's JSON writes it. A section that cannot be read (a refusal of the store or of `ont fn`, an answer without a member R50 to R52 name, the bound of R48) is one line in its place: `brief <intent> unreadable <code>`, `flow <intent> unreadable <code>`, `cost <intent> unreadable <code>` (`all` for the intent in R51), `api <month> unreadable <code>` or `capacity unreadable <code>`, the code as R22 passes it on (`store_answer_unreadable` for a missing member, `page_bound` for the bound); the page goes on. The form exits 0 once the reads of R4 answered; their failure is the form's refusal (R22), with nothing on standard output.

## Interface

`ap status [<key> [--requests]] [--text] [--store <path>]`, `ap status --term <word> [--text] [--store <path>]`, `ap status --brief <intent> [--store <path>]`, `ap status --wait <intent> [--timeout <seconds>] [--store <path>]`, `ap status --indicators [<intent>] [--text] [--store <path>]` and `ap status --page [--store <path>]`. Without `--text` one JSON object and a line break; with it plain lines (the wait has no `--text`; the brief and the page are plain lines and have none). The members are named in R4 to R16, R19, R21, R25, R35 to R42, R50 to R52.

Codes of `ap` itself: `usage`, `unknown_key`, `store_answer_unreadable`, `store_answer_unqualified`, `process_failed`; a refusal of the store passes on the store's code. `--page` adds no code of its own. `--indicators` and `--brief` add `unknown_intent` (exit 2). The wait adds `unknown_intent` (exit 2) and, as an error that ends it with exit 3, `policy_invalid` (the policy file) and `reading_failed` (`observe`'s) beside the store's and `process_failed`.

## Not included

- No report card, no chart, no cost in money but the lines of R50 and R51, read from the functions, no comparison across intents, no web page; no indicators of a unit of the old form (it has no unit object); no reason for a unit that waits on a constraint (`ap work` notes its `waits_on`), no wait on several intents, no push to the user.
- No view is read for a list: a view's cells are escaped and bounded; only the keys of the reviews and runs of a unit come from views (R16), and every text printed whole comes from `search` or `get`.
