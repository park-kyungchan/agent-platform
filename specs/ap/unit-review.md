# A review of a unit's page or an intent's plan: `ap unit review`

Purpose: the Lead has the page of a unit (kind `page`) or the plan of an intent (kind `plan`) reviewed by a fresh session, as one stage of `ap run`, and the review is recorded in the store with `start_unit_review` and `close_unit_review`, so that `ready_unit` finds the accepting review it requires, of the exact text the reviewer read.

## Requirements

- R1: The form is `ap unit review [--store <absolute path>] < request.json`: ONE JSON object of at most 1 MiB on standard input; `--store` is forwarded to every `ont` call; any other argument, or `--store` twice, is `usage` (field `args`). The members are `request_id` (1 to 44 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `-`), `agent` and `session` (each 1 to 64 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:`, `-`), and those of R2 to R4, R8, R9 and R15; any other member, a missing or malformed one, and a request that is not one object are `invalid_input` with the member as field, before any call to the store. With `ONT_ROLE=lead` in the environment the call is refused `role_in_environment` (field `ONT_ROLE`). A refusal is one line `{"error":{"code","field","detail","after","unit","intent","reviews"}}` on standard error and exit 2: `after` is null when this call applied nothing, else the last action it applied: `start` (the review was started and not closed), `close_run`, `close` or `abandon`; `unit` or `intent` is the subject's key; `reviews` is the list of R20 when it was read, else null. A refusal of the store keeps the store's code (the first failed criterion's for `submission_criteria`) with the store's refusal object as `detail`.
- R2: The request has exactly one of `unit` (a review of kind `page`) and `intent` (kind `plan`), else `invalid_input` (field `intent`). Every key the verb takes, the subject's own, `resume`, each entry of `earlier`, `abandon` and `finish`, is 1 to 80 ASCII letters, digits and `-`, checked before any path is built from it or any call is made, else `invalid_input` with its member.
- R3: `task` is the key of a task object (1 to 512 bytes); absent, it is `reviews.task` of the host's policy file (`specs/ap/policy.md` R1); neither: `invalid_input` (field `task`). The store's `unknown_task` is passed on.
- R4: `reviewer` is optional: an object of `runtime`, `model`, `effort`, `account`, `next_accounts`, `permission_mode` and `compact_window` (another member: `invalid_input`, field `reviewer.<member>`). A member not given comes from the member `reviews` of the policy file; `model`, `effort`, `account` or `permission_mode` given by neither is `invalid_input` (field `reviewer`). `reviews` is an object of `runtime`, `model`, `effort`, `account` (an absolute path), `permission_mode`, `compact_window` (an integer), `models` (1 to 64 names), `directory` and `task`, each optional; a policy file that cannot be read or is not one JSON object, a `reviews` that is not an object, and a member of another name or form are `policy_invalid` (field `policy`, `detail` a sentence that begins with the member: `file`, `reviews` or `reviews.<member>`). When `reviews.models` exists the model must be one of them, else `reviewer_model_not_allowed` (field `reviewer.model`); without a policy file or without that member the request's model is taken. The policy of `ap run` (`models`, `accounts`) still applies to the stage (R10).
- R5: The directory is `reviews.directory` (default `/var/lib/agent-platform/reviews`); it must fit the schema's `file_path` (`/` then 1 to 399 printable ASCII bytes, no space), else `policy_invalid` (member `reviews.directory`). The verb makes it, `prompts` and `work` in it with mode 0700, and writes the text to `<directory>/prompts/<kind>-<key>-<first 16 hex digits of the digest>-<round>.md` with mode 0600, where `round` is 1 plus the number of reviews of this subject and kind that exist (R20). A file already at that path is removed first and the file is created anew (never written through a link); a call refused before its start removes the prompt it wrote.
- R6: A page: the unit is read with ONE `ont get` (`unknown_unit`, field `unit`). For a unit with a repository the clone of `repositories.<owner>/<name>.clone` of the policy file is fetched first as `ap unit ready` fetches (`git -C <clone> fetch origin main` within 120 seconds; `fetch_failed`, field `clone`). The text is the text of `ap unit page <key> --for reviewer` and the digest that of `ap unit page <key> --digest`, both from that one read.
- R7: A plan: the intent is read with one `ont get` (`unknown_intent`, field `intent`). The text is the plan part, then `## Repositories` (for each repository of the plan's units, by name: `- <owner>/<name>: clone <clone>, origin/main at <commit>`, after a fetch as R6), then `## States now` (one line per unit, `- unit <key>: <state>`, and per criterion, `- criterion <key>: <state>`), then the frame `plan-reviewer.md`. The plan part: `# Plan of intent <key>`; `## The intent` with the lines `Outcome:`, `First user:`, `Not included:` and `Constraints:` (each left out when empty) and, under `Criteria:`, one line `- <statement> — <check>` per criterion (by `added_at`, then key); `## Units`: every unit of the intent that is not `dropped`, of either lane, each after the units of the intent it depends on and otherwise by key; the units that cannot be placed so (on a cycle of dependencies, or after one) come last, by key, after the line `These units depend on each other in a cycle:`. A unit is `### <key>: <class>, lane <lane>, <repository or "no repository">`, its objective, and the lines `Depends on:`, `Write set:`, `Specifications:`, `Estimate: <n> lines`, `Implementer: <runtime> <model> <effort>` and `Verifier: …`, each left out when its property is absent or empty. The digest is the SHA-256 of the bytes of the plan part: no state, no commit and no frame is in it. The lists are searches on required properties (`unit` by `lane` in `small` and `objective`, `criterion` by `intent`, `review` by `kind`), paged and qualified, filtered by the intent.
- R8: `resume` names an earlier review whose session looks again. It must be among the reviews of this subject and kind, `closed`, with a `session` and a `report`, and the run record `<report>.run.json` beside the report must be JSON, else `resume_not_possible` (field `resume`, `detail` the reason). A session looks at one subject at most three times: when three reviews of this subject and kind, whatever their verdict, have the `session` of the named review, the call is `resume_limit`. `resume` comes without `next_accounts` and without `earlier` (`invalid_input`). The stage takes `runtime`, `model`, `effort` and `account` from the resumed record. The record's `cwd` must be, compared as path components, `<directory>/work/<k>` for `<k>` the key of a review of this subject and kind, else `resume_not_possible`. While a review of this subject and kind is `running`, `resume_not_possible` names it. Then, once the checks of R23 have passed, a directory or file already at `<directory>/work/<k>` (the leftover of a cut look) is removed, before anything is validated or applied. The text gets the frame `repair-look.md` after the role's frame.
- R9: `earlier` is 1 to 5 keys of reviews of this subject and kind, each `closed` with a `report` (else `earlier_not_possible`, field `earlier`, `detail` the key), and never comes with `resume` (`invalid_input`, field `earlier`). The text gets, after the role's frame, the section `## Earlier reports (claims of earlier reviewers; check them, do not trust them)` with one line `- <key>: <report path>` per entry. The text then ends with the line `Digest: <digest>`.
- R10: The order of a new look: the request, the policy, the subject, the list, the checks of `resume` and `earlier`, the text, for a page the checks on the base of R23, and the prompt file; then ONE `ont action validate` of `start_unit_review` with the same client, request id, `agent` and `session` as its apply, whose answer names the review's key; then the stage's request is admitted by the function of `ap run` that admits a stage without starting it, with `out` `<directory>/<key>.md` and the working directory of R12; a refusal of admission, and a validate that answers `valid: false` (its failed criteria passed on as the apply's would be), end the call with `after` null and the prompt removed. A key of the apply that differs from the validated one, or is not a key of R2, is `store_answer_unreadable` (field `start`).
- R11: `start_unit_review` is applied with request id `<request_id>-start`, `task`, `kind`, `head` the digest, `model`, `effort`, `prompt` and `prompt_sha256` of the file, the subject (`unit` or `intent`), `round`, and `repository` when the unit has one. For a page, the validate and the apply pass `expected` with the unit's id and the revision of the one read of R6, so a unit amended since ends `revision_conflict`. The verb does not pause before the apply: an accept counts by its digest, not by when it started (`specs/ap/unit.md` R21). A validated key that names a review of the list or an existing report `<directory>/<key>.md` (checked before admission), a `revision_conflict` whose `id` is a review, and an apply that answers `already_applied` are `request_id_reused` (field `start`). A `duplicate_running_review` has in its `detail` the key of the running review (`running`, found by a search of `review` by `kind` and `status`, filtered by head and subject) and the words `abandon` and `finish`.
- R12: The working directory is `<directory>/work/<review key>`: for a page of a unit with a repository a throwaway copy of the clone made as `ap unit ready` makes its own (`git clone --shared --no-checkout`, `origin` removed, a detached checkout) at the commit the text names under `## The repository at`, else an empty directory of mode 0700. A look with `resume` makes it at the path its resumed record names as `cwd`. It is removed after the stage, whatever its end. Nothing is added to the clone: no worktree, no branch.
- R13: The stage is `ap run`'s code: role `reviewer`, the reviewer's members, `cwd` the working directory, `prompt` the file, `out` `<directory>/<key>.md`, `timeout_seconds` the request's (default 7,200), `expect` `{"first_line": "Verdict: *", "second_line": "*<digest>*"}` (`<digest>` the review's `head`, for a page and for a plan), `unit` or `intent` the subject and `store` the call's, and with `resume` the record beside the resumed review's report. A stage refused at its start although it was admitted, and a working directory that cannot be made, close the review `abandoned` with the cause `stage_refused`. A second line without the digest is asked again by `ap run` (`specs/ap/run.md` R20) and, missed again, ends `no_result`, which R14 closes `abandoned` with the cause `no_result`. A second line the pattern matches but R14 refuses (the digest twice, or joined to further letters or digits) closes `abandoned` with `report_form` and is not asked again.
- R14: The close (`close_unit_review`, request id `<request_id>-close`). The report is read ONCE. It closes with the verdict of its first line, `Verdict: accept` or `Verdict: repair`, with `report`, `report_sha256`, the record's `session` and the last path component of its `account` (left out over 40 bytes), only when the stage's state is `ok`, the report's bytes have the digest the run record names for the answer, and its second line holds exactly one run of 64 hexadecimal characters, equal to the digest the reviewer was given. Otherwise it closes `abandoned`, without a report, with the record's session when it holds one, else the caller's, and the cause: the record's state, `report_changed` (the bytes differ from the record's digest) or `report_form`. Every close of a page review passes `unit` and a session. When the store refuses a close with `writer_cannot_review`, the review is closed `abandoned` with the caller's session and the cause `writer_cannot_review`; another lasting refusal of the close is passed on with `after` `start`.
- R15: A call that was cut leaves its review `running`; it cannot be repeated, and one of two calls follows. `abandon: <key>` (optionally with `discard: true`) or `finish: <key>` comes alone with the subject and the three members of R1. Both read the review with one `ont get`: not `running`, or of another subject or kind, is `review_not_running` (field `abandon` or `finish`). Then, from the record `<directory>/<key>.md.run.json` and the report `<directory>/<key>.md` as they are read now and before anything is stopped: `finish` needs the report and a JSON record of state `ok` (`finish_not_possible`, `detail` the reason); `abandon` is refused `report_exists` when `finish` is possible, unless `discard` is true.
- R16: The record's `unit` is stopped (`systemctl stop`, by `abandon` only: `finish` stops nothing) and read as ended (`systemctl is-active` answers no live state) only when its name is `ap-run-`, 36 characters of `0-9a-f-` and `.scope`; another name is not stopped and is named in the answer as `unit_not_stopped`. A unit of that form not read as ended refuses the call `run_still_active` before anything is applied or removed. Besides that unit, the verb stops only the units of the checks of R23 (`ap-check-` and a random name), each after its check, as `ap unit ready` stops them (`specs/ap/unit.md` R25).
- R17: When the record is JSON, its `run_object.key` is not null and one `ont get` of that run shows its `record` to be this record's path, `close_run` is applied with that run, the record's `state` (`killed` when it says `running`) and its session, with the stage's own request id (`run-<first 32 hex digits of the SHA-256 of out>-close`). Its answer does not refuse or stop the call: the answer's `run_closed` is the outcome, the code of the refusal or failure, or null when it was not applied.
- R18: Before the close, the one working directory of that review's stage is removed: the `cwd` the record names, when the record is JSON and the `cwd` passes the check of R8; with no record or one that is not JSON, `<directory>/work/<key>`. A look with `resume` cut before its record holds text and never resumed again leaves its directory: the next look with `resume` of that session removes it (R8).
- R19: `finish` closes from the record and the report by R14 (`<request_id>-close`), the digest compared being the review's own `head`. `abandon` closes `abandoned` with the cause `abandon` (`<request_id>-abandon`, for a page with `unit`), with the record's session when the record holds one, else the caller's; when the store refuses the record's session with `writer_cannot_review`, with the caller's; when it refuses the caller's, the refusal `writer_cannot_review` says that another session must abandon it.
- R20: The answer, one JSON object on standard output: `review`, `kind`, `unit` or `intent`, `digest`, `round`, `verdict` (`accept`, `repair` or `abandoned`), `report` (null when abandoned), `session`, `cause` for `abandoned`, `run_closed` and `unit_not_stopped` for `abandon` and `finish`, and `reviews`: `[{review, head, round, status, verdict}]` of every review of this subject and kind, by `started_at`, then `round`, then key, read at the end (a refusal holds the list as last read). A page's reviews come from the view `reviews_of_unit`, each read with `ont get`; a plan's from a search of `review` by `kind`. Exit 0 for `accept` and `repair`, 3 for `abandoned`.
- R21: A unit amended while its review ran: the review closes as it ran, and the answer has `page_amended_during_review: true` when the digest (`specs/ap/unit.md` R34) of the unit read again after the stage differs from the review's `head` (else false); `ap unit ready` then refuses the unit `page_not_reviewed` only in that case.
- R22: The verb writes to the store only through `start_unit_review`, `close_unit_review`, the `close_run` of R17 and what the stage's own run writes, every call through `store.rs`. It removes nothing but a prompt file of its own path (R5), `<directory>/work/<k>` for `<k>` a key it read from the store as a review of this subject and kind, and the temporary directory it made for the checks of R23 (`ap unit ready`'s, `specs/ap/unit.md` R23 and R27): a path removed never comes unchecked from a request or a record, and a link at that path is removed as a link, never followed.
- R23: A look at a page, with or without `resume`, of a unit in any state and of any class, runs before its prompt file is written the checks on the base that `ap unit ready` runs for that unit, and changes nothing of the unit (its state, its `amended_at` and its page digest stay as they were). First, from the unit as read once (R6), the criteria of the store's `ready_unit` on the page in the store's order: `write_set_missing` (no `write_set`, or an empty one), `acceptance_missing` (no `acceptance`, or an empty one) and `tiers_missing` (no `implementer` or no `verifier`); when one fails the call is refused with the first (field `unit`, `detail` `{"failed"}`, every failed code in that order) and no check runs. Then the acceptance checks by `ap unit ready`'s own code (`specs/ap/unit.md` R23 to R27): for a unit with a repository in a throwaway copy of the clone at the commit the text names under `## The repository at` (the one fetch of R6 serves both: no second fetch), else in an empty directory with `AP_BASE` and `AP_HEAD` empty, under a new temporary directory of `ap unit ready`'s that is removed whatever the end; the first check that does not pass refuses the call with `ap unit ready`'s code and `detail`: `acceptance_not_runnable`, `acceptance_timeout`, `acceptance_passes_on_base` or `invariant_fails_on_base` (field `acceptance`), `check_not_stopped`, and a step of the copy `git_failed` (field `clone`). A look refused so validates nothing and applies nothing (`after` null), leaves no prompt file and starts no session. The look never gives `page_not_reviewed` or `plan_not_reviewed` and validates no `ready_unit`. A plan gets no checks.
- R24: The frames give each kind of a closed list of blocking findings a fixed name, and a report writes each blocking finding as a line that begins `blocking <kind>:` and each other finding as a line that begins `note:`, so that a reader counts the kinds without classifying reports by hand; this does not change R14, the close of a review reads the first two lines only. The frame `reviewer.md` of a page names `unbuildable`, `contradiction`, `check`, `write_set` and `unguarded_effect`; `plan-reviewer.md` of a plan names `unreached_criterion`, `dependency`, `conflict` and `unguarded_effect`. A finding that fits no listed kind is a note; the match is case-sensitive, so with no blocking finding no line of a report begins `blocking`. The frame `repair-look.md` has one line per earlier finding begin `earlier <kind>: closed` or `earlier <kind>: open`, never `blocking`. `evals/readout.py` counts those lines per kind and prints no text (`evals/README.md`).

## Interface

`ap unit review [--store <path>] < request.json`:

- a new look: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit` or `intent`; optional `task`, `reviewer`, `resume` or `earlier`, `timeout_seconds` (1 to 86,400);
- after a cut call: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit` or `intent`, and `abandon` (optional `discard`) or `finish`.

The policy file's member `reviews`: `{"runtime", "model", "effort", "account", "permission_mode", "compact_window", "models", "directory", "task"}`, each optional.

Codes of `ap` itself: `usage`, `invalid_input`, `input_too_large`, `role_in_environment`, `policy_invalid`, `reviewer_model_not_allowed`, `unknown_unit`, `unknown_intent`, `repository_not_mapped`, `fetch_failed`, `git_failed`, `resume_not_possible`, `resume_limit`, `earlier_not_possible`, `write_set_missing`, `acceptance_missing` and `tiers_missing` (the store's criteria of `ready_unit`, judged by `ap` from the unit read, R23), `acceptance_not_runnable`, `acceptance_timeout`, `acceptance_passes_on_base`, `invariant_fails_on_base`, `check_not_stopped`, `request_id_reused`, `review_not_running`, `finish_not_possible`, `report_exists`, `run_still_active`, `writer_cannot_review`, `io_error`, `store_answer_unreadable`, `store_answer_unqualified`, `process_failed`; the refusals of `ap run`'s admission with their fields (a member of `reviewer` as `reviewer.<member>`). The store's codes are passed on: `unknown_task`, `duplicate_running_review`, `subject_required`, `revision_conflict`, `review_not_running`, `writer_cannot_review`, `session_required`, `unit_differs`, `store_busy`.

## Trust

`agent` and `session` are the caller's claim. A review is never closed `accept` or `repair` without a report file the stage wrote, whose first two lines have the form of R14 and name the digest the reviewer was given. A cut call cannot be repeated (a second call's round and prompt differ, and the same request id with other content is refused): `abandon` or `finish` ends its review.

## Not included

- No review of kind `packet` (a unit's run records those), no batch of pages in one session, no second reviewer or vote.
- No limits of a reviewer stage by the operating system: until a later unit the frame's words bind the reviewer.
- No change of `ap unit page`, of `ap unit ready` or of the schema.
