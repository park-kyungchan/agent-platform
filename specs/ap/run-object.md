# A stage of `ap run` that belongs to a unit or an intent: the `run` object, the role and the author

Purpose: a stage that works for a unit or an intent is one `run` object of the store (who ran, on what model, how long, how many tokens) that opens before the stage's first launch and closes after its end, every stage carries its role to the engine as the actor's claim, and a stage may commit under its own name, without the store ever stopping, failing or holding a stage for more than 30 seconds at each end.

## Requirements

- R1: The request gains five optional members. `unit` and `intent` are each a key of 1 to 64 characters of ASCII letters, digits and `._-`; both together refuse `invalid_input` (field `intent`). `store` is an absolute path and only with `unit` or `intent` (else `invalid_input`, field `store`). `git_author` is a boolean (`false` is the same as absence). `ont_role` is only `verifier` and only for the role `reviewer` (else `invalid_input`, field `ont_role`). Every one of these refusals comes before any effect.
- R2: The request's `env` may not name `ONT_ROLE`, `ONT_AGENT`, `GIT_AUTHOR_NAME`, `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`, `GIT_COMMITTER_NAME`, `GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL`, `BASH_ENV` or `ENV` (`env_forbidden`, field `env`); a stage gets neither `BASH_ENV` nor `ENV` from the caller's environment either.
- R3: Every stage, with or without `unit`, is started with `ONT_ROLE` set to its role (`implementer`, `fixer`, `reviewer` or `writer`, or `verifier` when `ont_role` says so) and with no other role: no request and no variable of the caller's environment makes it `lead` or leaves it out. A page's writer has `ONT_ROLE` `writer`, and `writer` is the `role` of R7's `start_run`.
- R4: Every stage is started with `ONT_AGENT` set to `<runtime>:<model>` with every character outside `[A-Za-z0-9._:-]` replaced by `-` and the whole cut to 64 characters, so that the engine accepts it.
- R5: With `git_author` true the stage's environment gets `GIT_AUTHOR_NAME` = `<role> <model>` and `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL` = `<role>.<model>@agents.invalid`, where in `<role>.<model>` every character outside ASCII letters, digits, `.` and `-` is replaced by `-`; the role is the finer one of R3, and `GIT_COMMITTER_NAME` and `GIT_COMMITTER_EMAIL` get the same two values, for every runtime and role, so that no commit of a stage names the host (`user@host`, Git's default committer). Without it nothing of Git's environment is set.
- R6: A unit or an intent the store does not have refuses the call before any effect (`unknown_unit` field `unit`, `unknown_intent` field `intent`). Does not have means exactly one `ont get` that answered `unavailable`; any other answer or failure of that read does not refuse. The read comes after every refusal of admission, the policy's included (so a request the policy refuses makes no call to the store), and before the call claims `out`.
- R7: For a request with `unit` or `intent`, after admission (the policy step included) and after the call claimed its `out`, before the first launch only, `start_run` is applied through the store module with the reference, `role` (the finer one of R3), `runtime`, `model`, `effort`, `account` (the last path component of the first launch's account), `session` (only when known before the launch: a runtime that takes its id from `ap run`, or a resume), `record` and `stream` (the paths of the run record and of the raw stream, each left out unless it is a `file_path` of the schema), the client `ap`, the agent `ap-run`, the role `dispatch` (a claim of the driver, `specs/ap/roles.md` R3: the run's `role` parameter stays the stage's) and the request id `run-<first 32 hex digits of the SHA-256 of the absolute path of out>-start`. A move to another account applies nothing. The policy step stays the last check of admission: a stage it refuses makes no call to the store.
- R8: The record `<out>.run.json` holds `run_object`: null without `unit` and `intent`, else `{"key","start","close"}`, where `start` is `applied`, `already_applied`, `applied_late` or the code of the refusal or failure, `key` is null when the start failed, and `close` is null until the close was tried. The record written before the launch already holds the key when the start answered.
- R9: After the stage ended, whatever its end and after the loop over accounts, `close_run` is applied (request id `...-close`, the role `dispatch` as R7's start), and the record is then written once more with `run_object`. `close_run` takes `state`, `session` and `served_by` of the record, its four token counts as `input_tokens`, `cache_read_tokens`, `cache_write_tokens` (the record's cache creation) and `output_tokens` (a count the record holds as null is left out), and `limit`: the record's limit reading as compact JSON when it is at most 300 bytes, else the one window with the highest utilization as `{"window","utilization","status","resets_at"}` (a reading that still does not fit is left out). A stage that moved to another account is one object, and `close_run` holds the last account's session.
- R10: The store does not fail or hold a stage. When a read, a start or a close is refused or the store cannot be reached, the stage runs and ends as it would, its state, exit code and answer (but for `run_object`) are those it would have, and the record says so in `run_object`: a store held (busy or out of reach) at the start leaves no `run` object, one held at the end leaves a `run` with status `running`. Each of the three calls is one attempt bound to 15 seconds, so the longest delay is 30 seconds before the launch (the read and the start) and 30 after the end (the repeated start and the close).
- R11: A start that gave no answer (its bound, or a failure that is no refusal) may have been applied: it is applied once more after the stage ended and before `close_run`, with the same request id and exactly the parameters of the first call as they were before the launch. `already_applied` or `applied` gives the key; `applied` makes `run_object.start` `applied_late` (the object's `started_at` is then the stage's end, and the record's `start` is the true time).
- R12: A second call on the same `out` is refused `out_exists` before any call to the store, and the first call's object is untouched.
- R13: A request without the five members behaves as before: no call to the store, the record and the answer as before but for `run_object: null`, and the fallback record of a call whose files cannot be opened keeps its three members and has no start.
- R14: The close of an orphan. A request with the member `close` (the key of a `run` object, of the form of R1's keys), optionally `store` (R1's form), and no other member (`invalid_input` with the member's name, or with `close` or `store` for a malformed one) is no stage: it reads the object with one `ont get` (`unknown_run`, field `close`, when the store does not have it); an object whose `status` is not `running` answers `{"run": <key>, "closed": false}`, exit 0, before its record is read. An object with no `record`, a record missing or unreadable or not one JSON object, one whose `run_object.key` is not the object's key, or one whose `unit` is not a scope name of the form `ap-run-<36 of 0-9a-f->.scope` is refused `record_mismatch` (field `close`) and nothing is applied. Then the call reads the scope with `systemctl is-active` and the record's modification time and size, pauses 10 seconds and reads both again; only when both readings answer a state outside the five in which something of a unit may live (`active`, `activating`, `deactivating`, `reloading`, `refreshing`), neither call failed or answered nothing, the record's modification time is at least 60 seconds before the first reading, and the second reading gives the same modification time and size, it applies `close_run` with `state` the record's (`killed` for `running`, `failed` when it has none), `session` the record's when it has one of 1 to 200 bytes, and no `reason` (the schema declares none), under the request id `run-<first 32 hex digits of the SHA-256 of out>-close`, `out` the record's path without `.run.json`, as the agent `ap-run` with the role `dispatch` (`specs/ap/roles.md` R3; as `specs/ap/unit-run.md` R7 closes a stage), and answers `{"run": <key>, "closed": true, "reason": "orphan"}`, exit 0. Any other reading refuses `run_still_active` (field `close`): a stage's record is younger, or changes, before its scope exists, between its launches, while it closes and in the seconds after its CLI exits. Refusals are exit 2 on standard error, `{"error":{"code","field","detail"}}`; a store call (the read or the apply) that fails answers that form with the store's code (the engine's for a refusal), field `close`, exit 3; nothing else is written, and the call launches nothing.
- R15: From schema version 17 `start_run` takes the optional parameters `host` (a reference to a `host`) and `uses` (a reference to an `account`), which its rule sets as the run's properties of those names, and `start_install` the optional parameter `host`, which its rule sets as the install's `host`; no criterion reads them. Every `start_run` of R7 also carries `host`, the reference of the host `local` (the host `ap run` and its stage run on, `specs/ap/resource.md` R3), and, exactly when it carries `account`, `uses`, the reference of the `account` whose key is that `account` (the key `specs/ap/resource.md` R8 observes); R11's repeat carries the same parameters.
- R16: From schema version 19 a stage's run is also measured by `ap measure sessions` (`specs/ap/measure.md`): `measure_run` sets its `days`, `refusals` and `measured_at`, and a session no `ap run` started is a run of `state` `session`, made by `record_session` and extended by `extend_session`; what `ap run` applies and records is unchanged (its `start_run` and `close_run` carry none of the new parameters, and no pass changes the role, status, times or token counts they wrote).

## Interface

`unit` and `intent` name an object of the store at schema version 11; `store` is given to every `ont` call of the run as `--store`, as the older verbs give theirs; without it the calls reach the default store. A stage killed with its `ap run` leaves a `run` with status `running`: that is the truth the store holds, and the unit that comes back closes it, or the Lead, after it stopped the recorded scope by its name, with R14. `ap run` handles no signal (the standard library has none and `forbid(unsafe_code)` holds): after a signalled `ap run` the scope lives to its bound unless stopped.

Refusals, all exit 2 before any effect: `invalid_input` (fields `unit`, `intent`, `store`, `git_author`, `ont_role`; for R14 `close`, `store` and any other member's name), `env_forbidden` (field `env`), `unknown_unit`, `unknown_intent`, and for R14 `unknown_run`, `record_mismatch` and `run_still_active` (field `close`); R14's failed store call is exit 3 with the store's code.

The role and the author are claims, not proofs: an account's settings file or an MCP server's configuration can set the variables again, and the member `role` of an action request wins over `ONT_ROLE`, and a stage applies nothing: the driver claims `dispatch` for its run (R7, R9, R14); the engine logs the actor's role and does not authenticate it among callers of root (`specs/ap/roles.md` R6).

## Not included

- No sweep that closes a run left `running` by itself (R14 closes one object the Lead names), no indicator read from the objects, no change to the schema.
- No change to `ap unit`: its stages get `unit` and `git_author` in later units.
- No check that `unit`, `intent` or `store` belong together: the store answers.
