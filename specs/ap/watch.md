# The watch: `ap watch`

Purpose: a failed unit, a red delivery, a stale backup, a changed login setting, an expiring Claude sign-in, a high usage limit, a red check, a local PC the checks could not reach or GitHub drift, a changed runtime CLI flag, host artifact drift (an inventoried file, a unit file delivery installs, or a configuration file of `host/etc/` that differs on the host), or the API credit (a month's spend near a stop, a batch run left unsettled, a key file others can read) is seen without anyone looking, because one pass of `ap watch`, run by a timer every five minutes, reads ten detectors and keeps one finding of the store per signal: it opens it, sees it again, reopens it, and closes it when the signal is gone. The Lead reads the findings in `ap status` (a finding of severity `act` ends its wait) and triages them.

## Requirements

- R1: The form is `ap watch`, one pass, then exit; or `ap watch --record-login --totp-file <absolute path> [--replace]` (R3), its words in any order, each once. Any other argument (a word not of these, a word twice, `--totp-file` without an absolute path, `--totp-file` or `--replace` without `--record-login`) is `invalid_usage` (field `args`), exit 2, with nothing done: no directory, no lock, no call. `ap watch --help` is answered by `main.rs` before the verb. Neither form reads standard input.
- R2: The watch directory is `/var/lib/agent-platform/watch/` (the command line passes it; `watch::execute` takes it as an argument, so a test uses its own). It is made when absent (mode 0700). Both forms open `pass.lock` in it (made with mode 0600 when absent, never through a link) and take an exclusive `flock` without waiting; when another call holds it the answer is `{"status":"watch_busy"}` on standard output, exit 0, with nothing else done. The directory holds the lock, `state.json` (R8) and `login.json` (R3); a pass writes no other file.
- R3: `--record-login` writes `login.json` from the reading of R14 and the given path, and prints the object it wrote on standard output, exit 0: `{"recorded_at", "totp_file", "parts": {<part>: <value>}}`. When `login.json` is present it refuses `login_recorded` (field `login.json`, exit 2) unless `--replace` is given. A TOTP path that `stat -c %F` does not print as a regular file (absent, a directory, a link) is refused `totp_file_absent` (field `totp_file`, exit 2). A reading that cannot be made (R15) is `login_unreadable` (exit 3) and writes nothing. The TOTP file is read by `stat` alone, never by `System::read`. Only the Lead runs it: once after the install, and after a change of the login path made by the user's procedure.
- R4: A pass runs the detectors in the order `delivery`, `login`, `services`, `host`, `records`, `accounts`, `checks`, `runtimes`, `drift`, `credit`. Each either reads its sources and yields a set of signals, or is `unreadable` with the cause (a source could not be read). A signal is `{signature, kind, severity, summary, evidence}`; `signature` is a short readable text unique within the detector, and the summary begins with it. The finding of a signal has the native key `watch-<detector>-<the first 16 hex digits of the SHA-256 of the signature>` (only `a-z0-9-`, 27 to 31 bytes, whatever the signature holds) and the `detector` property `watch:<detector>`. The kind is `security` for every signal of `login`, `drift` for every signal of `drift`, `incident` for the others.
- R5: Per signal, by the present state of its finding (one search of the store per detector: the findings whose `detector` is `watch:<detector>`): absent, `open_finding` with the kind, detector, summary, evidence and severity; `open` or `planned`, `see_finding_again` with the evidence only when the severity differs from the stored one (with the new severity) or its `last_seen_at` is more than 57 minutes before the pass; `closed`, `reopen_finding` with the evidence; `dismissed`, nothing. So a signal seen in every pass opens one finding, and records nothing more within an hour unless its severity changes.
- R6: Per detector that read its sources: every finding whose `detector` is `watch:<detector>`, whose state is `open` or `planned`, and whose key this pass did not yield gets `close_finding` with the reason `not seen by ap watch <detector> at <UTC time of the pass>`, unless its family is unknown (R7). An `unreadable` detector closes nothing and yields one signal of its own, signature `unreadable`, severity `act`, the cause as its evidence. No finding whose `detector` does not begin with `watch:` is read for closing or changed: the search names the detector, and a finding of the answer whose `detector` is another is dropped.
- R7: The families of each detector, by signature prefix: `delivery`: `stale`, `end`, `checkout`; `login`: `no baseline` and one `login <part>` per part of R14; `services`: `failed`, `timer`; `host`: `disk floor`, `slice memory high`, `apt`, `old cli`, `pc down`; `records`: `backup stale`, `backup copy stale`; `accounts`: `sign-in`, `limit`; `checks`: `main red`, `dependabot`, `settings`; `runtimes`: `cli`; `drift`: `drift`, `inventory`; `credit`: `stop`, `unsettled`, `key mode`; and in every detector `unreadable`, a family always known when the detector reads. A finding's family is the longest family of its detector that is a prefix of its summary. A source that reads but does not hold a family makes that family unknown, and its findings are neither seen nor closed: `checkouts` absent or null; no `login.json` for every `login <part>`; no `state.json` member for a signal that compares with it (`slice memory high` without the counter, `apt` without the newest start, `end` while it is unknown whether an `end failed` is kept, R10); `memory.events` or the apt history absent; a `build_busy`, `busy` or `attempt_running` refusal under 30 minutes (R10); no policy file for `accounts` and `checks` (R20); no policy file, or a policy without `api`, for every family of `credit` (R31); no policy file, a policy that does not parse, or no reading of R30 in the last 60 minutes, for `pc down`.
- R8: `state.json` holds what the previous pass read, one member per detector that keeps one (`delivery`, `host`, `records`, `runtimes`). When it is absent or does not parse (or its member is absent), the signals that compare with it yield nothing and this pass's reading becomes their baseline. It is replaced by a rename of a temporary file (mode 0600) at the end of every pass, after its actions; the member of an `unreadable` detector, and the part of a member whose family is unknown, are carried over unchanged.
- R9: Every action goes through `store::unit_action` (as `ap work`'s do) to the host's store, agent `ap-watch`, role `watch` (`specs/ap/roles.md` R3), session `ww-<16 hex>`, request ids `ww-<the same 16 hex>-<n>` (`n` from 1 within the pass; the hex drawn from the kernel's random UUID per pass). A summary is cut at 600 bytes and an evidence at 2,000, on a character boundary.
- R10: `delivery` reads `/var/lib/agent-platform/deliver/last.json` (delivery's state directory, `deploy/deliver.json`): its modification time (`stat -c %Y`) and its record. Signals: older than 30 minutes by the modification time, `stale` [act]; `end` not `current` or `installed`, `end <end> <code>` (`-` for a null code) [act; `urgent` when `end` is `failed`, a rollback that did not restore]. A refusal coded `build_busy`, `busy` or `attempt_running` signals only when `state.json` shows the same `end` and code first seen more than 30 minutes before the pass; while it is under 30 minutes (or has no earlier reading) the family `end` is unknown. `state.json` keeps an `end failed` (its code) until an end `installed` or `current`: while it is kept, `end failed <code>` holds as `urgent` and the refusals `pair_differs`, `suppressed` and `held_by_rollback` yield no signal of their own. Whether one is kept is unknown after a baseline (no `state.json` or no `delivery` member) that read another end, until an end `installed`, `current` or `failed` is read: until then the family `end` is unknown, those three refusals yield no signal, and `state.json` holds no member `failed`. Each entry of `checkouts` whose result is neither `forwarded` nor `current`, `checkout <path> <result>` [act]. A `last.json` that is absent, unreadable or not one JSON object, or a `stat` that fails, makes `delivery` `unreadable`.
- R11: `services`: each unit `systemctl --failed --plain --no-legend --no-pager` lists, `failed <unit>` [act], except `ap-deliver.service` and `ont-backup.service`, which `delivery` and `records` cover; its evidence holds only `systemctl show`'s `Result`, `ExecMainStatus`, `StateChangeTimestamp` and `InactiveEnterTimestamp`. The timers of the fixed intervals `ap-deliver` 10 minutes, `ap-quota` 30 seconds, `ont-backup` 60 minutes, `ap-watch` 5 minutes and `ap-work` 60 seconds (`ap-work` only while its timer's `LoadState` is not `not-found`), read by one `systemctl show -p Id -p LoadState -p ActiveState -p LastTriggerUSec` of the timers and their services: a `LastTriggerUSec` older than three intervals while the service's `ActiveState` is not `active`, `activating`, `deactivating` or `reloading`, or an empty `LastTriggerUSec` while the host's uptime (`/proc/uptime`) exceeds three intervals, `timer <name>` [act]. A time is read in the form `<weekday> <YYYY-MM-DD> <HH:MM:SS> UTC` (the service sets `TZ=UTC`); another form, a failed call or a missing unit block makes `services` `unreadable`.
- R12: `host`: the free space of the file system of `/var/lib/agent-platform` (`df -BM --output=avail`) under 10,240 MiB, `disk floor` [act]; the `high` counter of `/sys/fs/cgroup/agents.slice/memory.events` rose within the last 60 minutes, `slice memory high` [info] (`state.json` keeps the counter and the time it last rose; a lower counter is no rise); a `Start-Date` in `/var/log/apt/history.log` newer than the newest `state.json` keeps, `apt <start>` [info] (one pass: the next pass's baseline holds it); a process whose command name is `claude`, `muse`, `agy` or `codex` and whose elapsed time exceeds seven days (`ps -eo pid=,etimes=,lstart=,comm=`), `old cli <pid> <start>` [info]: listed, never signalled or killed, its evidence only pid, command name, start and elapsed time, never its arguments. `pc down` is read after these sources (R30).
- R13: `records` reads `/var/lib/agent-platform/backup/last.json` (`scripts/backup-store.sh`). `state.json` keeps `backup_at`, the `at` of the last reading whose `backup` is a path, and `copied_at`, the `at` of the last reading whose `remote` is `copied`, `unchanged` or `none`; at the baseline each is that reading's `at` when it qualifies, else the pass's own time. A reading whose `remote` is `none` (no remote configured) counts for `copied_at` as a copy does, while `failed` counts as not copied. `backup_at` more than two hours before the pass while the store's action log has an applied action after it (`ont log` of `{"outcome":"applied","since":<backup_at + 1 s>,"limit":1}`: a row or a withheld count), `backup stale` [act]; `copied_at` more than 24 hours before the pass, `backup copy stale` [act]. A `last.json` that is absent or without its `at`, or an `ont log` that fails, makes `records` `unreadable`.
- R14: `login` compares the reading of these parts with `login.json`: `sshd -T` (the SHA-256 of its output with its lines sorted; a non-zero exit records `exit <code>`, run as `sh -c 'sshd -T 2>/dev/null; echo "exit $?"'`); the directories `/etc/ssh/sshd_config.d/`, `/etc/systemd/system/ssh.socket.d/`, `/etc/sudoers.d/`, `/etc/systemd/system/ssh.service.d/` and `/etc/systemd/system/ssh.service.requires/`; the files `/etc/pam.d/sshd` and `/etc/sudoers`; `ssh.service drop-ins` and `ssh.socket drop-ins` (the path, type and SHA-256 of every file `systemctl show -p DropInPaths` lists); `ssh.service fragment` and `ssh.socket fragment` (`FragmentPath` exactly as `systemctl show -p FragmentPath` prints it, its type and the SHA-256 of that file); and `totp file`, the type `stat -c %F` prints for the TOTP path `login.json` names (`absent` when not found). A directory part is read by `stat -c %F <dir>` and, when that prints `directory`, by `find <dir> -mindepth 1 -maxdepth 1 -printf '%f\t%y\t%l\n'`: each entry by its name, its type and, for a symbolic link, its target text (a link's target is never read), a regular file also by its SHA-256. A directory `stat` does not find reads as the empty set, at `--record-login` and in a pass alike; a directory path `stat` prints as another type (a symbolic link to a directory included) is recorded as that type with no entries. A file part is read by `stat -c %F` and, when it is a regular file, its SHA-256; one `stat` does not find is `absent`. A `FragmentPath` or a listed drop-in that is empty or not a regular file (a masked unit's link to `/dev/null`) is recorded as such without a digest. Every change of a recorded value is a difference.
- R15: Only a directory that exists and cannot be listed, an entry `find` prints that cannot be read, a regular file that exists and cannot be read, a `login.json` that cannot be read or has no `totp_file` and `parts`, or a `systemctl`, `stat`, `find` or `sh` call that cannot run, times out (10 seconds), or fails other than by `stat` not finding the path (`No such file or directory`, `Not a directory`), makes `login` `unreadable`; every other state of a part is a reading. A path is compared as the text `systemctl` or `find` printed: no path is canonicalised. The TOTP file is never read by `System::read`, in a pass or at `--record-login`.
- R16: No `login.json` is `no baseline` [act], and every `login <part>` family is unknown. Else each part whose reading differs from its value in `login.json` is `login <part>` [urgent]. The evidence names the part and the SHA-256 of the part's recorded value and of its value now, each as `login.json` holds a value; no byte of a secret and no entry name is in it.
- R17: The answer of a pass is one JSON object on standard output: `{"at", "detectors": {<name>: {"state": "ok"|"unreadable", "signals": <n>}}, "opened", "seen", "reopened", "closed", "failed"}`; the four lists hold keys, `failed` holds `{key, code}` (the code of the store's refusal, or the failure's; a search of a detector that fails is `{key: "watch:<detector>", code}`, and that detector acts on nothing; a `state.json` not written is `{key: "state.json", code: "io_error"}`). An `unreadable` detector counts its one signal. Exit 0 when every action applied, 1 when any failed, 2 for invalid usage; a refusal of R1 or R3, or a failure before the reading (the directory, the lock, the random UUID: exit 3), is `{"error":{"code","field","detail"}}` on standard error.
- R18: `deploy/ap-watch.service` is `Type=oneshot` with `ExecStart=/usr/local/bin/ap watch`, `Nice=10`, `Environment=TZ=UTC`, `Environment=HOME=/root`, a `PATH` that holds `/usr/sbin` (for `sshd`), and no `Restart=`. `deploy/ap-watch.timer` has `OnCalendar=*:0/5`, `AccuracySec=1s`, `Persistent=false` and `WantedBy=timers.target`. `ap deliver` installs the two files (`specs/ap/deliver.md` R48 to R50), and the section "The watch" of `deploy/README.md` describes them.
- R19: A pass writes only finding actions to the store and files under its watch directory. Its calls are `stat` (also of the key file of R31, by `stat` alone), `find`, `sh` (for `sshd -T`), `systemctl show` and `systemctl --failed`, `df`, `ps`, `gh` (`run list`, `api` GET), `python3 -I -B .../github/apply --check`, `git -C ... rev-parse`/`show`/`cat-file`/`ls-tree`/`for-each-ref`, `/usr/local/bin/ap --version` (R28), `/usr/local/bin/ont --version` (R33), `tailscale ssh <hosts.pc.ssh>` (the read-only digest probe of R33, with only `TAILSCALE_ENV`), `<cli> --version`/`--help` (for `claude` and `agy`) and `ont` (`search`, `log`, `action apply` of the four actions of R5 and R6); it never stops, starts, restarts, kills or signals a process or a unit, never writes a host file elsewhere, never fetches or writes in a checkout, fetches, checks out and writes in no clone either (delivery's clones of R28 and R29 included), and never reads a secret's bytes (except `<dir>/.credentials.json` under R21, whose non-expiry members are dropped in memory; the key file of R31 is never read).
- R20: `accounts`, `checks`, `runtimes`, `drift` and `credit` run after `records` in that order under R4–R6, with no new verb, argument or file beyond `state.json`. With no policy file, the families of `accounts`, `checks` and `credit` are unknown (no signal, nothing closed); a policy file that does not parse makes all three `unreadable`.
- R21: `accounts` (`sign-in`, kind `incident`): for each unique directory the policy's `accounts` member names under a key beginning `claude`, `<dir>/.credentials.json` is the one exception to R19, read whole by `System::read` and parsed in memory; only the integer `claudeAiOauth.refreshTokenExpiresAt` (milliseconds) is kept, every other member is dropped into no log, evidence or state, and any file failure is reported as a fixed code, never parser text. `sign-in <dir>`: end within 72 hours after the pass [act], or past [urgent]; absent file, non-JSON, or missing/non-integer member: `sign-in <dir> absent` [act]. Evidence holds only the end as UTC to the second (or the fixed code when absent).
- R22: `accounts` (`limit`, kind `incident`): for each `account` value of `run` objects whose `started_at` is within 6 hours of the pass (one `ont search` of `run` with `ge started_at`), for each window name, the newest reading of that window among the account's runs whose `limit` text parses as a JSON object with a `windows` object or a `utilization` number; when any of them is at least 0.9, `limit <account>` [info], with evidence holding the window, the value and the run's key. A failed search makes `accounts` `unreadable`.
- R23: `checks` (`main red`, kind `incident`): for each repository of the policy's `repositories` member, `gh run list -R <repository> --branch main --limit 20 --json workflowName,status,conclusion,headSha,createdAt`; the newest completed run of each workflow name concluded other than `success` or `skipped` is `main red <repository> <workflow>` [act], with evidence holding the workflow, the conclusion and the head's first 12 hex digits.
- R24: `checks` (`dependabot`, kind `incident`): for each repository of the policy's `repositories` member whose entry in `/home/palantirkc/platforms/agent-platform/github/repositories.json` sets `vulnerability_alerts` true, `gh api repos/<repository>/dependabot/alerts?state=open&per_page=100` holding at least one alert is `dependabot <repository>` [act], with evidence holding the count and highest severity, never an advisory's text. A 404, or a 403 whose `message` is `Dependabot alerts are disabled for this repository.`, is `off` (no signal); any other 403 is a failed call.
- R25: `checks` (`settings`, kind `incident`): one run of `python3 -I -B /home/palantirkc/platforms/agent-platform/github/apply --check --file /home/palantirkc/platforms/agent-platform/github/repositories.json`: each difference whose `wanted` is not null is `settings <repository> <setting>` [act], each whose `wanted` is null is `settings <repository> <setting>` [info]; a repository answered `refused` yields `settings <repository> classic protection` [act] beside its differences; a repository answered `failed`, an exit 2, an exit 1 without that JSON answer, any other exit, or any failed or timed-out `gh` call makes `checks` `unreadable`.
- R26: `runtimes` (kind `incident`), for `claude` and `agy`, the two runtimes `ap run` starts: `state.json` keeps the first line of `--version` and the SHA-256 digest of the runtime's fixed flag list. When either differs from the kept entry or none is kept, the pass reads `<cli> --help`, checking that every flag in the fixed list appears on a help line after leading spaces (alone or after `-x, ` or `--y, `) followed by a character that is neither alphanumeric nor `-`, or end of line. `--version` failure or missing CLI is `cli <name> absent` [act]; `--help` failure or timeout is `cli <name> help` [act]; each missing flag is `cli <name> flag <flag>` [act]. The kept entry is replaced only when every flag is found. No model runs; no session starts.
- R27: `drift` (kind `drift`): reads the inventory `<K>:host/deployments/host-artifacts.json` of the commit K the installed `ap` names (R28, R34) from delivery's clone, `git -C /var/lib/agent-platform/deliver/agent-platform show` (no fetch, no write); a failed read or non-array of `{source, destination, sha256}` objects makes `drift` `unreadable`. A row's `destination` is relative to `host/`. A row whose `destination` is not a relative path under `deployments/` without `..`, or whose `host/<destination>` is not a `blob` at K (`git -C <clone> cat-file -t <K>:host/<destination>`), or whose `source` is not an absolute path, yields `inventory <destination>` [act] without reading the host file. Otherwise `System::read` hashes `source`: different SHA-256 is `drift <destination>` [act]; absent is `drift <destination> absent` [act]; any other read failure makes `drift` `unreadable`. Evidence holds only the digests and K, never a byte of the file.
- R28: `drift` also reads, in the same pass, the unit files delivery installs (`specs/ap/deliver.md` R48 to R50). K is the commit the installed file names: one call `/usr/local/bin/ap --version`, whose first line is `ap <version> <40 hex>`. The unit files of K are those of `specs/ap/deliver.md` R48, read from delivery's clone `/var/lib/agent-platform/deliver/agent-platform` (`git -C <clone> ls-tree <K> deploy/`, then `git -C <clone> show <K>:deploy/<name>` for each); the test of a unit file's name and mode has one home, `crates/ap/src/deliver.rs`, which `drift` calls. The host's copy is `/etc/systemd/system/<name>`, read through `System::read`: other bytes, `drift deploy/<name>` [act]; not found, `drift deploy/<name> absent` [act]. A version line that names no commit, a `git` call that fails, or any other read failure makes `drift` `unreadable`. Evidence holds only the two SHA-256 digests (or `absent`) and K, never a byte of a file.
- R29: `drift` also reads, in the same pass, the files of `host/etc/` at K (R34, R35) that delivery installs (`specs/ap/deliver.md` R71 to R74), from delivery's clone `/var/lib/agent-platform/deliver/agent-platform`: the entries of `git -C <clone> ls-tree -r -z <K> host/etc/`. Each entry's path is named without `host/` (`host/etc/x` is `etc/x`). An entry that is a blob of mode 100644 or 100755 whose path is made of ASCII letters, digits, `/`, `.`, `_`, `@` and `-`, with no empty, `.` or `..` component, is compared: the host's file is `/<path>` (`host/etc/agent-platform/policy.json` is compared with `/etc/agent-platform/policy.json`), its bytes against `git -C <clone> show <K>:host/<path>`; other bytes, `drift <path>` [act]; not found, `drift <path> absent` [act]. Any other entry is `inventory <path>` [act], and its host file is not read. A `git` call that fails or any other read failure makes `drift` `unreadable`. Evidence holds only the two SHA-256 digests (or `absent`) and K, never a byte of a file.
- R30: `host` (`pc down`, kind `incident`, as R4 gives every `host` signal): the family is read only when the policy file parses and names `hosts.pc` (an object); it is the policy reading the pass already makes (one read of the file per pass). A policy without `hosts.pc` makes the family known with no signal (an open `pc down` closes, and neither record is read); no policy file, or one that does not parse, makes it unknown (R7) and does not make `host` `unreadable`. Its sources are two records, read whole through `System::read` at their default paths only: the checks log `/var/lib/agent-platform/checks.jsonl` (`specs/ap/check.md` R15) and the door's record `/var/lib/agent-platform/door.jsonl` (`specs/ap/door.md` R6), because a stage's call that the door places on the PC leaves no row in the checks log. A record that does not exist adds nothing; one that exists and cannot be read makes `host` `unreadable` (R4); a line that is not one JSON object with the members below is skipped, never a cause of `unreadable`. A checks-log line is at its `end` (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`): `host` `pc`, or `reason` `pc_busy`, `pc_memory_low` or `pc_engine_differs` (the probe answered, `specs/ap/check.md` R4 and R19), is `reached`; `reason` `pc_unreachable` or `pc_lost` is `down`; any other line is no reading. A door line counts only with `verb` `check` and is at `at` plus `ms` divided by 1,000 rounded down: an integer `exit` is `reached`; `reason` `pc_busy`, `pc_memory_low` or `pc_engine_differs` is `reached`; `reason` `pc_unreachable` or `pc_lost` is `down`; any other is no reading. The reading of the pass is the newest reading whose time is at most 3,600 seconds before the pass (a later time counts); at an equal time `reached` wins. `down`: the signal `pc down` [act]. `reached`: no signal. No reading: the family `pc down` is unknown, so an open finding is neither seen nor closed. The signature is `pc down`; the summary begins `pc down: ` and says that the local PC took no check, and to ask the user to run `Start-ScheduledTask -TaskName ap-checks-keepalive` in PowerShell on the PC, then to read `tailscale ping <h>` on this host, `<h>` the part of `hosts.pc.ssh` after its `@`. The evidence holds only the record's file name (`checks.jsonl` or `door.jsonl`), the reading's time as UTC to the second, and for a checks-log line its `host` and `reason`, for a door line its `reason`: never `dir`, `repository`, `head`, `command`, `run`, `role`, `name` or a `pc_*` member. The family keeps nothing in `state.json`, and the watch never writes, truncates or locks either record.
- R31: `credit` (kind `incident`, as R4 gives it): the API credit of `specs/ap/api.md`. It reads the pass's one reading of the policy file (R30) with the reader of `specs/ap/api.md` R3 (`config` of `crates/ap/src/api.rs`). No policy file, or a policy for which that reader answers none (no member `api`), makes every family of `credit` unknown (R7): no search, no `stat`, and an open finding is neither seen nor closed. A policy file that does not parse, an `api` that reader answers `policy_invalid`, a failed search, or a `stat` that fails other than by not finding the path (`No such file or directory`, `Not a directory`) makes `credit` `unreadable`. Else, from one search of `run` by `batch_runs` of `crates/ap/src/api.rs` from 0 (every run of role `batch`, every page), which both families below read: `stop standard` and `stop soak` [act], when the amount R9 of `specs/ap/api.md` counts for the pass's UTC month (`tally` over `month_of` of the pass, summed as `sum` sums it) is at least 80 % of `stops_usd.<queue>` (counted_usd_micros >= stop x 800,000), evidence the month, the counted and the stop in micro-USD; `unsettled <run key>` [act], for each run whose `status` is not `closed` and whose `started_at` is more than 26 hours before the pass, evidence its key, `job`, `started_at` and `reserved_usd_micros` (a run closed without a settlement counts at its reservation in `stop` and yields no `unsettled`: nothing settles it later). `key mode` [urgent]: one `stat -c '%a %F'` of `api.key_file` (a link is not followed) that prints a mode other than `600` or a type other than a regular file, evidence the mode and the type; a key file `stat` does not find yields no signal. The key file is read by `stat` alone, never by `System::read`: no byte of it is in an evidence, an answer or `state.json`. `credit` keeps nothing in `state.json` and changes no finding whose detector is not `watch:credit`.
- R32: The `subject` of a finding (`ontology/README.md`, Version 24). Every `open_finding`, `see_finding_again` and `reopen_finding` that R5 applies for a signal of the detector `host` (R12) carries the parameter `subject`, the host `local`, but the one for the signal `pc down` (R30), whose subject is the host `pc`; `close_finding` (R6) and every action for a signal of another detector carry none. If `tailscale ssh` fails or times out (PC offline), no drift signal is emitted. ## Interface

`ap watch` and `ap watch --record-login --totp-file <path> [--replace]`; the answers of R3 and R17. `crates/ap/src/watch.rs` holds the verb, the lock, the actions, `state.json` and the fixed runtime flag lists; `watch/detect.rs` the detectors `delivery`, `services`, `host` and `records`; `watch/login.rs` the parts of the login path; `watch/accounts.rs`, `watch/checks.rs`, `watch/runtimes.rs` and `watch/drift.rs` the four detectors of R20–R29 (`drift` with its three sources: the inventory of R27, the unit files of R28, the configuration files of R29); `watch/credit.rs` the detector `credit` of R31. The install, the state and the undo are in `deploy/README.md`, "The watch".

## Not included

- The detector `work`; the weekly audit workflow; the sign-in end of Antigravity; a fetch of any repository; rows not yet added to `host/deployments/host-artifacts.json`.
- "with no session activity" for an old CLI process (it is listed by age only).
- Any automatic repair, restart or notification outside the store.
- `apt <start>` and `slice memory high` are short-lived by design: the first holds for one pass, the second for 60 minutes after the last rise; each is closed by the pass that no longer sees it.
- While the timer is stopped, nothing is watched; a `timer ap-watch` finding is raised by the next pass after it restarts.
- A pass that falls between the installer of `ap` and the unit step of one call of `ap deliver`, or between the install of a commit's `ap` and its configuration step's write, can report a file that the call then writes; the next pass closes the finding.
- Starting or mutating the PC by the watch (the user starts the task); R33 runs only the read-only digest check of the checks account's `ont`, a signal for a PC that answered but was busy or low on memory, and the paths `AP_CHECKS_LOG` and `AP_DOOR_LOG` name (the watch reads the default paths).
- A repair, restart or fetch by the watch; a drift of a drop-in or of a file `deploy/` does not name.
