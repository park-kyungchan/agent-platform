# The routing map: which tier may take which kind of work

Purpose: routing is data with bars. The host's policy holds, for each kind of work and size, the lowest implementer and verifier tier, and a bar on a tier's stop-and-repair rate; a unit whose tier is under the map, or whose implementer tier passed its bar on that kind, is refused when it is added, amended or made ready and is not started by the dispatch pass, so a weak agent is never given work the measurements say it does not finish. A nightly pass reads the rates from the function `flow` by tier (`specs/ap/measure.md` R17) and opens the finding that bars a tier, or closes it when the rate came back under the bar. Without the member nothing is refused, held or applied. The code: `crates/ap/src/unit/routing.rs` (the reader and the three tests) and `crates/ap/src/measure/routing.rs` (the pass).

## Requirements

- R1: The policy's optional top-level member `routing` (`specs/ap/policy.md` R21) is an object of exactly `order`, `large_lines`, `map` and `bar`. `order`: 2 to 20 pairs `[runtime, model]` (two texts of 1 to 80 bytes each, as a unit's tier holds them), weakest first, no pair twice. `large_lines`: an integer from 100 to 5,000. `map`: an object from a unit kind of the schema (the value type `unit_kind`) or `"default"` to `{"small": {"implementer", "verifier"}, "large": {"implementer", "verifier"}}`, each value a pair of `order`; `"default"` is required. `bar`: `{"rate", "window_days", "min_merged"}`, `rate` a number above 0 and at most 1, `window_days` an integer from 1 to 92, `min_merged` an integer from 1 to 100. Any other form (a member missing or of another name, a value out of its form, a tier of the map not in `order`) is `policy_invalid`, field `policy`, with a `detail` that begins with the member (`routing`, `routing.order`, `routing.order.<i>`, `routing.large_lines`, `routing.map`, `routing.map.<kind>` and below, `routing.bar` and below).
- R2: `ap policy check` validates the member with its reader `routing`, listed after `client` (`specs/ap/policy.md` R12, R13): a file without the member passes it; a member of another form is refused as R1 says, with the reader `routing`.
- R3: `ap unit add` and `ap unit amend` check a unit that has a `kind` and both tiers, as the op leaves it (for `amend`, the unit as read with the request's members in their places), after their other checks (the refusals of `specs/ap/unit.md` R2 to R16, the unit's existence for `amend`, the dependencies of R16) and before the apply. The policy file is read as `ap work` reads it (`specs/ap/work.md` R2): a file that is absent, or that has no member `routing`, means no check; one that cannot be read or is not one JSON object is refused `policy_invalid` (field `policy`), and so is a member of another form (R1). The size is `large` when `estimate_lines` is at least `large_lines`, else `small` (a unit without `estimate_lines` is small); the row is `map[kind]`, else `map.default`. A unit without a kind, or without one of its tiers, is not checked and the store is not searched for it.
- R4: A bar is live for `(runtime, model, kind)` when a finding with the key `routing-bar-<runtime>-<model>-<kind>` is in the state `open` or `planned`. The live bars of a check come from one search of findings by the detector `ap-routing` (`eq` on the required `detector`, every page, each qualified); a finding whose key does not begin `routing-bar-` is no bar. A search that fails refuses the op with the store's code, nothing applied.
- R5: The three refusals, each exit 2 with `{"error":{"code","field","detail"}}`, the implementer's tier tested before the verifier's, so that when both fail the implementer's is reported; for one tier, in this order: a tier whose pair `[runtime, model]` is not in `order` is `tier_not_in_order` (field `implementer` or `verifier`, detail `{"order": <the policy's order>}`); a tier that stands before the row's in `order` is `tier_below_map` (the field, detail `{"lowest": [runtime, model]}`, the row's pair); an implementer tier with a live bar for the unit's kind is `tier_over_bar` (field `implementer`, detail `{"finding": <its key>, "lowest": <the first pair at or after the row's in order that has no live bar for this kind, or null>}`). Each refusal so names the lowest tier that would pass. The verifier's tier has no bar.
- R6: Only the Lead lifts a bar: by `close_finding` (the next pass opens it again while the rate stays above the bar, R11) or by `dismiss_finding`, which waives the bar for good: the pass never changes a dismissed finding. A bar the pass opened, saw again or reopened stays live until the Lead or a later pass (R11) closes it.
- R7: `ap unit ready` runs the three tests of R5 on a `draft` unit with the codes of R5, after its other checks (the write set, the stored digest, the page review: `specs/ap/unit.md` R20 to R22, R44) and before the acceptance checks run on the base (R23), with the policy read and the search of R3 and R4 (`specs/ap/unit.md` R51). A unit that is not `draft` is not tested.
- R8: The dispatch pass `ap work` runs the three tests on a `ready` candidate before it starts it, right after the delivered dependencies (`not delivered: <key>`, `specs/ap/work.md` R26), so after the validate of `start_unit`, with one search of findings a pass, taken at the first candidate that has a kind and both tiers and reaches the test. A candidate that fails is not started and gets `waits_on` `tier over bar: <key of the finding>`, `tier below map` or `tier not in order`, and the pass goes on to the next candidate (`specs/ap/work.md` R27). A member `routing` of another form refuses the pass `policy_invalid` before the lock. So a unit added before the map existed, or whose tier passed its bar after its last amendment, starts no stage on that tier. The tests are not part of the run's own checks (`drive::checked`): a direct `ap unit run`, a merge candidate (R22 of `specs/ap/work.md`) and a stage started before the bar opened are not tested.
- R9: `ap measure routing [--day <YYYY-MM-DD>] [--store <path>]`, a pass of `specs/ap/measure.md` R1 in the form of `ap measure kpis` (`specs/ap/measure.md` R14, R18): each option at most once (another argument list is `usage`, field `args`), `--store` an absolute path, `day` the pass's UTC day by default, a day of another form or after the pass's day `invalid_input` (field `day`), each exit 2 before any effect. Then the policy file is read as R3 reads it (`policy_invalid` exit 2); without the member `routing`, or without a policy file, the pass reads nothing of the store, applies nothing and prints `{"at","day","skipped":"no routing"}`, exit 0. The window is the `bar.window_days` days before `day`, `day` itself not in it.
- R10: The sums. One search of `intent` (every page); for each intent whose `status` is `confirmed`, in key order, the function `flow` with `by` `tier` (`specs/ap/measure.md` R17), called in the process with the intent and the window; the pass sums `merged` and `stopped_or_repaired` of the groups by `(runtime, model, kind)`, leaving out a group with a null in one of the three. Then one search of findings by the detector `ap-routing`. Every read comes before any effect: a search or a function that fails ends the pass with `{"error":{"code","field","detail"}}` on standard error, exit 3, nothing applied.
- R11: The findings. For each sum with `merged` at least `bar.min_merged`, its rate being `stopped_or_repaired` divided by `merged` and its key `routing-bar-<runtime>-<model>-<kind>`: when the rate is above `bar.rate` and the finding does not exist, `open_finding` (detector `ap-routing`, kind `incident`, severity `act`, a summary naming the tier and the kind, an evidence with the rate, the two counts and the window); when it is `open` or `planned`, `see_finding_again` with that evidence; when it is `closed`, `reopen_finding` with that evidence; when the Lead dismissed it, nothing. When the rate is not above the bar and the finding is `open` or `planned`, `close_finding`, its reason naming the rate, the bar, the two sums and the window. A sum under `min_merged` changes nothing. Each apply is made as the agent `ap-measure` with the role `measure`, under a request id fixed by the day, the action and the tier and kind (`routing-<day without dashes>-<opened|seen|reopened|closed>-<16 hex of the SHA-256 of runtime, model and kind>`); an apply that answers `already_applied`, or `request_id_reused` under that id, counts as `unchanged`, as `specs/ap/measure.md` R15 counts it, so a second pass of a day repeats no action of the first (it may see again a finding the first opened or reopened).
- R12: The answer and the bounds. The pass applies only `open_finding`, `see_finding_again`, `reopen_finding` and `close_finding`, and only to findings whose key begins `routing-bar-`. A refused or failed apply is counted in `failed` (`{finding, action, code}`) and does not end the pass. The pass prints one JSON line `{at, day, since, until, opened, seen, reopened, closed, unchanged, failed}`, the first four lists the keys applied to, `unchanged` a number, and exits 0 when `failed` is empty, else 3.
- R13: The timer. `deploy/ap-measure.service` runs the routing pass as its third step, after `ap measure kpis`; the sessions and KPI steps have the `-` prefix, so that a failed measurement does not keep the routing pass from running (`specs/ap/measure.md` R16).
- R14: The cost of the tests. The check of add, amend, ready and the dispatch pass adds no call that takes longer than one search of findings: it never calls the function `flow` (only the nightly pass does), reads the policy file once per op and searches the findings at most once per op or pass.

## Interface

The member: `{"routing": {"order": [[runtime, model], ...], "large_lines": n, "map": {"<kind>" | "default": {"small": {"implementer": [runtime, model], "verifier": [runtime, model]}, "large": {...}}}, "bar": {"rate": x, "window_days": n, "min_merged": n}}}`. The codes: `tier_not_in_order`, `tier_below_map`, `tier_over_bar` (exit 2 of `ap unit add|amend|ready`), `policy_invalid`; the dispatch pass's waits: `tier over bar: <key>`, `tier below map`, `tier not in order`. The pass: `ap measure routing [--day <YYYY-MM-DD>] [--store <path>]`. The finding of a bar: key `routing-bar-<runtime>-<model>-<kind>`, detector `ap-routing`, kind `incident`, severity `act`.

The undo: remove the member `routing` from the policy file; every unit then is added, amended, made ready and started as before, and the pass applies nothing. A bar's finding stays as the history it is.

## Not included

- The map's values on the host (the policy of the host is its own unit).
- Any routing of reviewers, of the Lead or of subagents.
- A stage on a CLI the user has suspended.
- A test of a direct `ap unit run`, of a merge run, or of a stage that started before its bar opened.
