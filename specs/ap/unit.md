# A unit's page as an object of the store: `ap unit add|amend|ready|drop|page|defer|undefer|used`

Purpose: the Lead writes the page of a unit as a `unit` of the store (schema version 25) instead of a page file with a block, makes it ready only after its acceptance checks ended on the base as their kinds require (and, for the classes `contract` and `effect`, after a review accepted the exact page), and every later stage of the unit is given the one text `ap unit page` renders from that object.

## Requirements

- R1: The forms are `ap unit add|amend|ready|drop [--store <absolute path>] < request.json` (the op is the first argument, the request ONE JSON object of at most 1 MiB on standard input) and `ap unit page <key> [--for implementer|verifier|reviewer] [--digest] [--store <absolute path>]` (a read, by arguments); `--store` is forwarded to every `ont` call; any other argument, an option given twice, and `--for` with `--digest` are refused `usage` (field `args`) before any call to the store. Two forms of `ap unit < request.json` stay: a request with a member `op` is refused `usage` (field `op`, `detail` `ap unit <op> < request.json: the op is the first argument`) before any other check of it, a request with `case` included; a request with a member `case` and no `op` is the case call (section "One case" of `crates/ap/README.md`). A request on standard input with neither member, or one that is not JSON, is refused as an argument list that reaches no verb (`specs/ap/verbs.md` R4).
- R2: Every writing op has `request_id` (1 to 44 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`), `agent` and `session` (each 1 to 64 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `:` and `-`) and the members of its op (R15 to R18); an unknown, missing or malformed member, and a request that is not one JSON object, is refused `invalid_input` with its field before any call to the store; a request over 1 MiB is `input_too_large`.
- R3: Each op applies one action without a `validate` before it, with the client `ap`, the request's `agent` and `session` as the actor, the request id `<request_id>-<op>` (`-add`, `-amend`, `-ready`, `-drop`) and the role `lead` (`specs/ap/roles.md` R3).
- R4: When the environment variable `ONT_ROLE` is `lead`, every writing op is refused `role_in_environment` (field `ONT_ROLE`) before any call to the store; `page` writes nothing and is not refused.
- R5: A refusal is one line `{"error":{"code","field","detail","after","unit"}}` on standard error with exit 2, with `fields` too for a refusal of R37: `after` is null when this call applied nothing, else the op's suffix (the action applied, `already_applied` included, and something after it failed); `unit` is the unit's key when the request names one as a string, else null. A refusal of the store keeps the store's code (the first failed criterion's code for `submission_criteria`) and has the store's refusal object, as the store answered it, as `detail`.
- R6: The key of a new unit (`unit` of `add`) is 1 to 59 bytes of lower-case ASCII letters, digits and `-`, beginning with a letter (the run's session `u-<key>` stays within the engine's 64 bytes of an actor's member); any other is `invalid_input` (field `unit`). Another key (`amend`, `ready`, `drop`, `page`, an entry of `depends_on`, `intent`, `origin`) is 1 to 512 bytes.
- R7: Every text is a string within the schema's byte bound: `objective` 2,000, `interface` 20,000, `invariants` 3,000, `not_built` 1,500, `notes` 3,000, `verifier_questions` 4,000, `report` 1,000, `repository` and `branch` 200, `reason` 600; a text over its bound is refused `invalid_input` with `detail` `{"bytes","limit"}`, never cut. An empty `objective` or `reason` is the store's to refuse. `lane` is `small` or `objective`, `class` `plain`, `contract` or `effect`; `implementer` and `verifier` are each exactly `{runtime, model, effort}`, three strings of 1 to 80 bytes; `estimate_lines` is an integer of at least 0.
- R8: The whole action request (`action_type`, `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `parameters`, as sent) is at most 60,000 bytes, else `page_too_large` (field `page`, `detail` `{"bytes","limit"}`): the engine takes 65,536.
- R9: `repository` is `<owner>/<name>`: two nonempty parts of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`, the name not `.` or `..`.
- R10: `branch` is at most 100 bytes of ASCII letters, digits, `.`, `_`, `/` and `-`, does not begin with `-` or `/`, holds no `..`, has no first component `origin`, `refs`, `heads`, `remotes` or `tags`, is not `main` or `HEAD`, and is a name `git check-ref-format --branch` takes (no empty component, no component beginning with `.` or ending with `.lock`, no `/` or `.` at the end).
- R11: `write_set` is at most 60 patterns, each a string of 1 to 200 bytes without a control character, of one of two forms: an exact path (components joined by single `/`, each not empty, none `.` or `..`, none holding `*`, `?` or `[`, so no leading `/`, no final `/` and no `//`), or `<dir>/**`, where `<dir>` is an exact path by that same rule. Nothing else is a pattern: `crates/ap/src/*`, `**`, `a/**/b.rs`, `a/**/`, `a/`, `./a`, `a/*.rs`, `a/b?.rs`, `a/[b]/**` and `/a` are each refused `invalid_input` (field `write_set.<i>`, rule `form`; the entry that is refused first has `detail` `{"forms":["<path>","<dir>/**"]}`, R37). One function, `write_set_pattern` of `crates/ap/src/review.rs`, holds the grammar and the matcher's reading of a pattern: `add`, `amend`, the checks of R38 and R39, the matcher of `ap unit run` (`specs/ap/unit-run.md` R16) and the map lines of R32 call it. The rules `required`, `max_bytes` and `max_entries` are as before.
- R12: `read_first` is at most 40 entries of 1 to 300 bytes without a control character, each a path or `<path>:<from>-<to>` with `1` ≤ `from` ≤ `to`.
- R13: `specs` is at most 20 entries of 1 to 300 bytes without a control character, each a path without `#` or `<path>#R<n>`.
- R14: `acceptance` is at most 20 objects of exactly `{check, kind}`: `check` ONE line (no line feed or carriage return) of 1 to 600 bytes, a shell command; `kind` `fails_on_base` or `invariant`.
- R15: `add` has `unit`, `lane`, `class`, `objective` and any other page property (`intent`, `origin`, `interface`, `invariants`, `acceptance`, `not_built`, `write_set`, `read_first`, `specs`, `notes`, `verifier_questions`, `report`, `repository`, `branch`, `implementer`, `verifier`, `estimate_lines`, `depends_on`, `wave`) under its own name, and applies `add_unit`; a used key (the engine's `revision_conflict`) is refused `unit_exists` with the store's refusal as `detail`.
- R16: `depends_on` is at most 20 keys, each of a unit that exists, read with one `ont get` each after every other check and before the apply: a key the store does not have is refused `unknown_dependency` (field `depends_on`, `detail` the key), nothing applied.
- R17: `amend` has `unit` and any page property but `lane`, `intent` and `origin` (each refused `invalid_input` with its name), checked as in R7 to R14 and R16, and applies `amend_unit`; its answer has `page_review_outdated`, true when the unit's class read back is `contract` or `effect` and no review of kind `page` of the unit, `closed` with verdict `accept`, has a `head` equal to the digest of R34 of the unit as read back; else false (an amendment of `implementer`, `verifier`, `estimate_lines`, `depends_on` or `wave` alone keeps an accept).
- R18: `drop` has `unit` and `reason` and applies `drop_unit`. `amend` and `drop` read the unit with one `ont get` after the role check of R4 and before R16's reads and the apply: a unit the store does not have is refused `unknown_unit` (field `unit`, `after` null), as `ready` (R20) and `page` (R29) do.
- R19: After the apply of `add`, `amend` or `drop` the unit is read back with `ont get`, and the answer is one JSON object on standard output: `unit`, `outcome` (`applied` or `already_applied`), `state` and `amended_at` as read (and `page_review_outdated` for `amend`).
- R20: `ready` has `unit`. It reads the unit with one `ont get` (`unknown_unit` when the store does not have it) and gives `ready_unit` the unit's own `intent` when its lane is `objective`, and `page_digest`, the digest of R34 computed from that read, for every unit in any state and of any class. When the unit read is not `draft`, nothing is run and the apply's answer is passed on (a repeat answers `already_applied`, a new request `unit_not_draft`, the store's first failed criterion, with `page_not_reviewed` listed beside it when the digest has no accepted review).
- R21: A `draft` unit of class `contract` or `effect` needs a review of kind `page`, `closed` with verdict `accept`, whose `head` is the digest of R34 computed from the same read, whenever that review started; the keys come from the view `reviews_of_unit`, each review is read with `ont get`; else `page_not_reviewed` (field `unit`, `detail` `{"digest"}`) before anything runs.
- R22: For a unit with a repository, the clone is the member `repositories.<owner>/<name>.clone` (an absolute path) of the host's policy file (`specs/ap/policy.md` R1), read through the host's port: no file, no `repositories`, no entry or no `clone` is `repository_not_mapped` (field `repository`); a file that cannot be read or is not one JSON object, and a `repositories`, an entry or a `clone` of the wrong form, is `policy_invalid` (field `policy`, `detail` a sentence that begins with the member: `file`, `repositories`, `repositories.<owner>/<name>` or `repositories.<owner>/<name>.clone`). `models` and `accounts` are not judged here.
- R23: `ready` then runs `git -C <clone> fetch origin main` within 120 seconds (`fetch_failed`); the base is the commit the clone's `origin/main` then names. The checks run in a throwaway copy under a new temporary directory: `git clone --shared --no-checkout <clone> <tmp>/base`, `git remote remove origin` in the copy, a detached checkout of the base there. Nothing is added to the clone: no worktree, no branch. A step of the copy that fails is `git_failed` (field `clone`, `detail` the step). Without a repository the checks run in a new empty directory `<tmp>/base`, `AP_BASE` and `AP_HEAD` are empty strings and the answer's `base` is null.
- R24: The checks run one after another, each through the port's `call` within 330 seconds as `sh -c <wrapper> sh <output file> env -i -C <dir> PATH=<the caller's> HOME=<the caller's> AP_BASE=<base> AP_HEAD=<base> timeout -k 10 300 bash -c <check>`: the wrapper sends both outputs of the command into one file under the temporary directory and its standard input from `/dev/null`, and prints the command's status as its last line on a descriptor the command does not inherit (a check can neither print a status nor keep the call open through it; an output file that cannot be opened prints none). The check's status is that printed line and nothing else; a call that returns no such line has none. This plain form is the whole command only where the port's `units()` is false.
- R25: Where the port's `units()` is true, the wrapped command runs inside a named transient unit, in the view of `specs/ap/run-limits.md` R12: `systemd-run --wait --pipe --collect --quiet --expand-environment=no --unit ap-check-<random> --slice agents.slice -p RuntimeMaxSec=320s -p TimeoutStopSec=10s -- sh -c <wrapper> sh <output file> env -i -C <dir> … /usr/bin/bwrap … -- timeout -k 10 300 bash -c <check>`, the check's text one argument byte for byte. After every check, whatever its end, the unit is stopped (`systemctl stop`) and read as ended (`systemctl is-active` answers no live state) before the temporary directory is removed; a unit not read as ended is `check_not_stopped` (`detail` the unit's name).
- R26: The rule of a check's end: no status is `acceptance_not_runnable`; 124 or 137 `acceptance_timeout`; for kind `fails_on_base`, 125, 126, 127 or at least 200 is `acceptance_not_runnable`, 0 is `acceptance_passes_on_base`, any other status passes; for kind `invariant`, 0 passes and any other status is `invariant_fails_on_base`. The first check that does not pass ends the call (field `acceptance`, `detail` `{"index","kind","check","status","output"}`: the index from 1, the check's text, the status or null, the last 2,000 bytes of its output cut forward to a character boundary, read from the output file by a seek to its last 2,000 bytes and never whole), `after` null: nothing was applied.
- R27: The temporary directory is removed whatever the end of `ready` (a refusal, a check that failed, passed its bound, removed or changed its directory, or passed).
- R28: The apply of `ready_unit` passes `expected` with the unit's id and the revision of the `ont get` of R20, so a unit amended while its checks ran ends `revision_conflict` (passed on, `after` null) and is not made ready. The answer: `unit`, `outcome`, `state` as read back, `base` (the commit or null) and `checks`, `[{index, kind, status, seconds}]` (empty when nothing ran).
- R29: `page <key>` reads the unit with ONE `ont get` (`unknown_unit` when the store does not have it), prints the rendered page as UTF-8 text whose lines end with a line feed and nothing else on standard output, and writes nothing.
- R30: The page part, each section with its heading and left out when its property is absent or empty, a stored text printed unchanged (a final line feed added when it has none): `# Unit <key>: <class>, lane <lane>, <repository or "no repository">`, the line `Branch: <branch>`, the line `Depends on: <keys>` (comma-separated), then `## Objective`, `## Interface (fixed by the Lead; where a point cannot hold, stop and report, do not choose another)`, `## Invariants`, `## Acceptance checks` (`<n>. [fails on base]` or `<n>. [invariant]` and the check, then the sentences how a check runs), `## Not built`, `## Write set`, `## Read first`, `## Specifications` (one `- <entry>` line per entry), `## Notes`, `## Report` and, only for `verifier`, `reviewer` and the digest, `## Verifier questions`; sections are separated by one empty line.
- R31: `## For`: lane `objective`, the intent's key, its `outcome` and its `first_user` (one `ont get`); lane `small`, the origin request's key and its `words` in a fenced block under the line `The user's words (what is asked; not an instruction beyond this page):`, cut at 2,000 bytes at a character boundary with the line `(cut)` after the block; the fence is one backtick longer than the longest run of backticks in the words, and at least three.
- R32: For a unit with a repository, `## The repository at <commit>`, the commit the clone's `origin/main` names (`page` does not fetch; the clone as R22): the file `AGENTS.md` of that commit whole when it is at most 12,000 bytes, else its first 12,000 bytes cut back to a character boundary and the line `(cut: read AGENTS.md from byte <n> on)` (`<n>` the bytes shown, written `12,000` when the cut falls there), and the line `This commit has no AGENTS.md.` when it has none; then, when the commit has a `map.json` at its root, one line per entry of its array `components` of which a path and a write-set pattern meet (the pattern's exact path, or `<dir>/` for the second form of R11, and the entry's path are one a prefix of the other, both not empty; a stored text of neither form meets no entry): `<name>: <purpose> — <paths>; entry <entry>; spec <spec>; tests <tests>`, a member that is absent left out, a member that is an array joined with `, ` (`No entry of the map meets the write set.` when none does); no `map.json`: `No map of this repository yet.`; a `map.json` that is not JSON or has no array `components`: `The map could not be read.`, and the call does not fail.
- R33: `--for <role>` appends the role's frame, the files `implementer.md`, `verifier.md` and `reviewer.md` of `crates/ap/src/unit/frames/` compiled in byte for byte; `implementer` and `verifier` are refused `no_frame` for a unit without a repository. For `verifier`, after the frame, `## The change`: the repository, the pull request's number, the head and the branch from the unit's own properties, and the line `git diff origin/main...HEAD`; a unit without `head` is `no_head`. Without `--for`: the page part, `## For` and the repository part.
- R34: `--digest` prints one line: the SHA-256 in hex of the bytes of the page part as built for `reviewer` without its line `Depends on: ...`, ending with its last line feed. Nothing outside the unit's own page properties enters it: no `## For`, no repository, no frame; neither do the dependencies, the tiers (`implementer`, `verifier`) nor `estimate_lines`, so an amendment of only these keeps the digest. The text `--for` prints, for every role, still shows the line `Depends on: ...`. It reads nothing but the unit, and needs no clone.
- R35: Every call to `ont` goes through `store.rs` (an answer `store_busy` is asked again up to five times); the verb writes to the store only through `add_unit`, `amend_unit`, `ready_unit` and `drop_unit`, and `defer_unit`, `undefer_unit` and `record_use` (R50), writes no file but under its own temporary directory, and adds nothing to the clone but what `git fetch origin main` brings.
- R36: `ap unit review` runs the checks of R23 to R27 before a look at a page (`specs/ap/unit-review.md` R23) by this same code: the same temporary directory, copy, command, units, rule of a check's end and removal, at the commit its text names after its one fetch, and in the same order after the criteria `write_set_missing`, `acceptance_missing` and `tiers_missing` of the store's `ready_unit` and after the checks of R38, R39 and R46, which a look runs in that order by the same code `ready` calls; it runs neither the page review of R21 nor the apply of R28, and `ready` is unchanged by it.
- R37: A writing op runs every check of R2 to R16 that comes before any call to the store (the admitted members, `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`, every page property, `reason`, and every entry of `acceptance`, `write_set`, `read_first`, `specs` and `depends_on`) and then refuses once, naming every member that breaks one: `code` `invalid_input`, `field` and `detail` of the member the checks refuse first (as when it is the only one), and `fields`, an array in that same order of `{"field","rule","bytes","limit"}`, one per member. `field` is written as `field` is (`acceptance.1.check`); `rule` is the first rule the checks find the member breaks: `unknown_member` (R2, also a member of an acceptance check or a tier), `not_admitted` (`lane`, `intent` or `origin` in `amend`, R17), `required` (a refused member that is absent or empty), `one_of` (a string that is none of the values of `lane`, `class` or `kind`), `form` (a type, or a pattern of R6 and R9 to R13), `max_bytes` (a text over its bound whatever `detail` says, also `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, a key, a list entry and a tier's string: `bytes` its bytes, `limit` its bound, for `branch` the 100 of R10), `max_entries` (a list over its most entries: `limit` that number, `bytes` null) or `one_line` (an acceptance check with a line feed or carriage return); `bytes` and `limit` are null for every rule but these two. A member is named once (`lane` in `amend` is `not_admitted` only), and a member whose own form is wrong is not looked into (an `acceptance` that is not an array is one entry `form`). A refusal before any member is read (not JSON, not one object, over 1 MiB) and every refusal after these checks (`page_too_large`, `role_in_environment`, `unknown_unit`, `unknown_dependency`, the checks of `ready`, the store's) has no `fields`. The checks admit what they admitted before: an empty `objective` or `reason` is still the store's to refuse (R7).
- R38: `write_set_form`. `ready` of a `draft` unit, after the read of R20 and before the page review of R21 and before anything of R22 to R27: when one or more stored patterns are of neither form of R11, the call is refused `write_set_form` (field `write_set`, `detail` `{"patterns": [those patterns in the stored order, at most ten], "count": <their number>, "forms": ["<path>","<dir>/**"]}`, `after` null, no `fields`): no review is read, nothing is fetched, no check runs, nothing is applied. A unit that is not `draft` is not judged (R20). A stored entry that is not a string is skipped by this check and by R39's.
- R39: `plain_writes_protected`. A pattern can only match protected paths when it is an exact path that `specs/ap/unit-run.md` R36 protects, or `<dir>/**` whose `<dir>` is, or lies under, one of R36's trees (its first component, compared without case); `crates/ap/**` and `crates/**` can match others and are admitted. A unit of class `plain` with such a pattern is refused `plain_writes_protected` (field `write_set`, `detail` `{"patterns": [every such pattern in its order, at most ten], "count": <their number>, "needs": "class contract, lane objective"}`, `after` null, no `fields`), nothing applied. It is judged (a) by `add`, from the request's `class` and `write_set`, after the checks of R37, `page_too_large` and R4 and before any call to the store; (b) by `amend` only when the request has `class` or `write_set`: the class and the write set are the request's where it gives them, else the unit's as the one `ont get` of R18 read it, after `unknown_unit` and before R16's reads and the apply (a stored text of neither form is not judged there; R38 refuses it at `ready`); (c) by `ready` of a `draft` unit, after R38's check and before R21. A unit of class `contract` or `effect` is never refused by it.
- R40: `## Protected paths`. For a unit of class `plain` with a repository, the repository part of R32 ends, after its last line, with an empty line and the section `## Protected paths`, an empty line, the sentence line `This unit is of class plain: its run stops protected_path_changed when it changes one of these paths, compared without case, whatever its write set names. A page that needs one is class contract, lane objective.` and four lines: `- exactly: check, review.md`; `- the last component, in any directory: clippy.toml, .clippy.toml, rustfmt.toml, .rustfmt.toml, rust-toolchain, rust-toolchain.toml, build.rs, cargo.toml, cargo.lock`; `- the last two components, in any directory: .cargo/config.toml, .cargo/config`; `- the first component, the path itself or anything under it: ci, .github, specs, scripts, .cargo, deploy, etc, content, github, ontology, engine, host`. The names are those of the lists of the run's check (`specs/ap/unit-run.md` R36), lower-case, printed from them. The section is in every text that has the repository part (without `--for` and for each role, before the frame) and is absent for the classes `contract` and `effect` and for a unit without a repository. It is not in the page part: the digest of R34 is unchanged.
- R41: The wave. `add` and `amend` take `wave`, an integer from 1 to 99 (else `invalid_input`, rule `form`), and give it to the action under its name. It is no part of the page: neither the page part nor the digest of R34 holds it, and an amendment of `wave` alone keeps an accept.
- R42: The stored digest. `add` gives `add_unit`, and `amend` gives `amend_unit`, the parameter `page_digest`: the digest of R34 of the page as the action leaves it, for `add` computed from the request, for `amend` from the unit as R18's read gave it with the request's members in their places. That read is one `ont get` of the unit's head and revision, and the apply of `amend` passes `expected` with the unit's id and that revision: a unit changed since the read ends `revision_conflict` (passed on, `after` null).
- R43: The clauses. An entry of `specs` of the form `<path>#R<n>` whose path begins with `specs/` and ends with `.md` names the clause `<repository>:<path>#R<n>` of the unit's own repository (`specs/ap/clause.md`). `add` with a `repository` and one or more such entries, and `amend` whenever its request has `specs` or `repository` (the other one as R18's read gave it), give the action `clauses`: the references of type `clause` in the entries' order, each once; `amend` gives the empty list when none is left. `ap` reads no clause for this: a named clause need not exist. R8's bound is judged on the request as sent, with `page_digest` and `clauses`: `add` judges it once; `amend` judges it where the base does, with a `page_digest` of its length and the `clauses` the request alone names, and once more after R18's read (`page_too_large`, `after` null).
- R44: `ready` of a `draft` unit whose stored `page_digest` is present and is not the digest R20 computes is refused `page_digest_differs` (field `unit`, `detail` `{"digest","stored"}`, `after` null, no `fields`) after R39's check and before R21: no review is read, nothing is fetched, no check runs, nothing is applied. A unit without a stored digest goes on as before this requirement.
- R45: `## Clauses`. For a unit whose `clauses` is not empty, the text that has `## For` has, directly after that section and set off by one empty line as the sections are, the heading `## Clauses`, an empty line, the line ``Sentences of the specifications this page names; one with its tests and the units that name it: `echo '{"parameters":{"key":"<key>"}}' | ap fn clause` (through the stage's door).``, an empty line and one line `- <key>` per clause, in the stored order. It is no part of the page part (the digest of R34 is unchanged); a unit without clauses prints as before this requirement.
- R46: The citations of a page on the base. For a unit with a repository, at the commit B that the fetch names (R23 for `ready`, `specs/ap/unit-review.md` R6 for a look): (a) `read_first`: of each entry its first word, the text before its first space, or the whole entry when it has none; when the word ends with `:<from>-<to>` (two runs of ASCII digits, 1 <= from <= to) that is its range and the text before it its path, else the whole word is its path; one final `/` of the path is dropped. The path must name an entry of the tree of B (a file, a link or a directory), read from Git's objects in the clone (no checkout read, nothing added to the clone), else `read_first_missing`; a text that is no relative path of the tree (an absolute path; an empty, `.` or `..` component) names none and is `read_first_missing` without a call to Git; a path through a link names none. With a range the path must name a file of mode 100644 or 100755 that has at least `to` lines (its bytes split at line feeds, a final line feed ending the last line, a last line without one still a line, an empty file having none: `a\nb` has 2), else `read_first_range`. (b) `specs`: an entry `<path>#R<n>` whose path begins with `specs/` and ends with `.md` names the clause `<repository>:<path>#R<n>` (R43); unless `<path>` lies inside the write set that clause must be a `clause` object of the store, else `clause_missing`. (c) Every `clause` object of the store whose `repository` is the unit's, whose `path` lies outside the write set and one of whose `tests` entries (`<path>:<line>`, the path the text before its last `:`) lies inside it must be named by an entry of `specs`: `<path>#R<n>` of that clause, or an entry without `#` (one final `/` dropped) that equals the clause's `path` or is followed in it by `/` (`specs/ap` and `specs` name `specs/ap/unit.md`; `spec` and `specs/a` do not), else `clause_unnamed`. "Inside the write set" is the matcher of `ap unit run` (`specs/ap/unit-run.md` R16). The clauses of (b) and (c) come from ONE search of `clause` with `eq` on `repository` (every page, each qualified, else `store_answer_unqualified`, field `clause`; a refusal of the store is passed on as R5 says); a Git step that fails other than by not finding a path is `git_failed` (field `clone`, `detail` the step); a blob over the port's output limit fails its call, so a range on such a file is `git_failed`. ## Interface

`ap unit add|amend|ready|drop|defer|undefer|used [--store <path>] < request.json` and `ap unit page <key> [--for implementer|verifier|reviewer] [--digest] [--store <path>]`.

- `add`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`, `lane`, `class`, `objective`; optional `intent` (required by the store in the lane `objective`), `origin` (required by the store in the lane `small`), `interface`, `invariants`, `acceptance`, `not_built`, `write_set`, `read_first`, `specs`, `notes`, `verifier_questions`, `report`, `repository`, `branch`, `implementer`, `verifier`, `estimate_lines`, `depends_on` (keys), `wave` (1 to 99), `kind` (a value of `unit_kind`), `use` (1 to 600 bytes; required in the lane `objective`), `depends_delivered` (keys).
- `amend`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`; optional the members of `add` but `lane`, `intent` and `origin`.
- `ready`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`.
- `drop`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`, `reason`.
- `defer` and `undefer`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`, `by` (the key of a request).
- `used`: `request_id`, `agent`, `session`, `unit`, `evidence`.

The answers: `{"unit","outcome","state","amended_at"}` for `add` and `drop`, with `page_review_outdated` for `amend`; `{"unit","outcome","state","base","checks"}` for `ready`; `{"unit","outcome","state"}` for `defer`, `undefer` and `used`; the text of the page, or one line of 64 hex digits for `--digest`.

Codes of `ap` itself: `tier_not_in_order`, `tier_below_map`, `tier_over_bar` (R51), `usage`, `invalid_input`, `input_too_large`, `page_too_large`, `role_in_environment`, `unknown_dependency`, `unit_exists`, `unknown_unit`, `unknown_intent`, `unknown_request`, `page_not_reviewed`, `repository_not_mapped`, `policy_invalid`, `fetch_failed`, `git_failed`, `acceptance_not_runnable`, `acceptance_timeout`, `acceptance_passes_on_base`, `invariant_fails_on_base`, `check_not_stopped`, `specs_missing`, `design_missing`, `design_not_reviewed` (R53), `no_frame`, `no_head`, `write_set_form`, `plain_writes_protected`, `page_digest_differs`, `read_first_missing`, `read_first_range`, `clause_missing`, `clause_unnamed`, `store_answer_unreadable`, `store_answer_unqualified`, `process_failed`.

A refusal of the store passes on the store's code, such as `intent_required`, `intent_not_open_for_units`, `origin_not_current`, `small_must_be_plain`, `small_needs_one_repository_and_a_write_set`, `request_has_a_live_unit`, `objective_empty`, `depends_on_itself`, `unit_not_amendable`, `unit_not_draft`, `write_set_missing`, `acceptance_missing`, `tiers_missing`, `dependency_dropped`, `plan_not_reviewed`, `page_digest_differs`, `page_not_reviewed`, `unit_ended`, `reason_empty`, `deferred`, `unit_not_deferrable`, `by_not_the_users_word`, `unit_not_merged`, `use_not_named`, `evidence_empty`, `revision_conflict`, `request_id_reused`, `store_busy` or `invalid_parameter` (among others for a `write_set` pattern that R11 admits and the schema's `write_pattern` refuses, such as one with a space).

## What the checks on the base prove, and what they do not

- A check of kind `fails_on_base` that fails on the base shows that the page's check tests something the base does not have; it does not show that the change will make it pass. A check of kind `invariant` that passes on the base shows the rule holds before the change.
- A check must end within its bound of 300 seconds from a cold copy with no build output: what needs a build belongs to the repository's own check, which the unit's run waits for.
- The copy has no remote and no `origin/main`: a check names commits by `AP_BASE` and `AP_HEAD` only. On the base both are the base, so a check that compares `AP_BASE` with `AP_HEAD` compares a commit with itself: it may only be of kind `invariant`. A later unit runs the same checks on a head with `AP_HEAD` the head.
- A named test is checked by its result line (`cargo test … | grep -q '^test <name> ... ok'`), because a test filter that matches nothing ends with 0.
- A check sees only `PATH`, `HOME`, `AP_BASE` and `AP_HEAD` of the environment: its environment is clean, but the caller's is not secret from it, because the caller's process is readable through `/proc` by the same user (a check can read `/proc/<pid>/environ` of the caller and of its parents). What a check does to a path outside its directory (another repository, the clone through its own path, the host) is the page's fault and is not held until checks run under the operating system's limits; `ready` holds only its own temporary directory, the clone's worktrees and branches, and, where units are used, that no unit of a check is left running.
- Without units, a check that starts a process in a new session outlives the call; with units, stopping the unit ends every process of the check.

## Trust

`agent` and `session` are the caller's claim, as the engine's actor is. The page review of R21 is matched by the text of the page: a review of another text does not make the unit ready, even when the store's own rule (by the times) would take it.

## Not included

- No review of a page or a plan (`ap unit review`), no run of a unit, no approval, no map file: `page` reads a `map.json` when the commit has one.
