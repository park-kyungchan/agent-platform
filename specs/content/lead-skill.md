# The Lead skill: `content/skills/lead`

Purpose: the session the store names Lead of an intent loads `content/skills/lead/SKILL.md`, installed as `/root/.agents/skills/lead/SKILL.md`, and reads its references under `content/skills/lead/references/` where a step routes to them. The skill holds the Lead's whole view, its design mode and execution mode, its own limits and where the state is. Its design mode puts the contract of a change into `specs/` of the owning repository, as a spec unit reviewed and merged first, before the plan of the units that build it, so no Lead of any CLI goes from a confirmed intent to units without the Spec stage of the order of work (`specs/content/agents-file.md` R3, section 2).

## Requirements

- R1: `content/skills/lead/SKILL.md` is at most 6,500 bytes (`SKILL_BYTE_LIMIT` of `ci/check-content.py`, `file_over_limit`), begins with a `---` frontmatter of `name` and `description` holding no changing value or model name (`skill_without_frontmatter`, `changing_value_in_always_loaded_text`, `model_name_in_content`), and keeps its four numbered headings `## 1. The whole view`, `## 2. The two modes`, `## 3. Your own limits` and `## 4. Where the state is`, in this order.
- R2: In `## 2. The two modes`, `### Design mode` lists its steps in this order: `Request and intent`; `Analysis` (the design note `DESIGN-<topic>.md`); `Spec` (before the plan, put the contract in `specs/` of the owning repository as a spec unit of class `contract` and `kind` `instructions`, with the rows of the corpus's route table ROUTES.md (which `OFFICIAL-DOCS.md` section 4 names) it rests on cited as `path:line`, reviewed and merged first; or cite the existing `<path>#R<n>` in the units' `specs`); `Plan and pages`; and `Hand over`. The frontmatter's `description` names `a spec` between `a design` and `a plan`. The row `specs` of section 1 of `content/skills/lead/references/unit-page.md` notes that `specs` is required in the lane `objective` at `ap unit ready` (`specs_missing`, `design_missing`; `specs/ap/unit.md` R53).
- R3: A pull request that changes `content/skills/lead/SKILL.md` or `content/skills/lead/references/*.md` passes `ci/check-content.py`, passes `evals/cases/select` without unaccepted `lost_facts` (`evals/cases/README.md`), and passes the suites of `specs/content/agents-file.md` R9 before it is approved; after delivery, `/root/.agents/skills/lead/SKILL.md` equals the merged file by SHA-256.
- R4: In `## 2. The two modes`, `### Execution mode` holds five rules, each one sentence: a wave is run in a `session of its own`, which takes the Lead with `ap intent` op `lead` by the user's word routed to the intent (the word that named the Lead before, where the user gave no newer one); the Lead waits only in one blocking `ap status --wait <intent>`, `never by a repeated` `ap status` or by sleeping; in execution mode the stages do the work and the Lead starts no `subagent`; an amend sends `only the member` a refusal or a report names; a fact of the store is read through a view (`ont render`) or `ap status`, not through a search and a parser of the Lead's own. No sentence of `content/skills/lead/references/` tells a Lead in execution mode to start a worker or a subagent. `ci/check-content.py` refuses a `SKILL.md` whose `### Execution mode` lacks one of the words `session of its own`, `ap status --wait`, `never by a repeated`, `subagent`, `only the member` and `ont render` with `lead_execution_rule_missing` and the missing words (tested in `ci/test_check_content.py`).

## Interface

`content/skills/lead/SKILL.md` in the form of R1 and R2, and `content/skills/lead/references/unit-page.md` with the row of R2. `ci/check-content.py` holds the size, the frontmatter and its values of R1 (`file_over_limit`, `skill_without_frontmatter`, `changing_value_in_always_loaded_text`, `model_name_in_content`); the headings of R1 and the steps, the description and the row of R2 are held by the tests that cite them and by the verifier's reading. The words of R4 are held by `ci/check-content.py` (`lead_execution_rule_missing`); that each rule is one sentence and that the references start no worker in execution mode, by the verifier's reading. R3 is held by the gate, which the Lead runs, and by the readback after delivery; `./check` does not run the suites.

## Not included

- No change of the order of work in `content/AGENTS.md`: its section 2 already names the Spec stage; this skill is where the Lead does it.
- No check that a cited `path:line` of a row of ROUTES.md (which `OFFICIAL-DOCS.md` section 4 names) says what the specification takes from it: the reviewer of the spec unit reads the lines.
- No refusal in the skill itself: `ap unit ready` refuses a unit of the lane `objective` without `specs` or whose intent names no design (`specs/ap/unit.md` R53).
- The limits and the state of the skill are unchanged by R2 and R4.
- No refusal in `ap` of what R4 forbids: the rules bind the Lead's own turns; the session records show them in use.
