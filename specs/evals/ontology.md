# The Ontology Engineering suite: `evals/ontology/run`

Purpose: what a model can do in Ontology Engineering from the corpus of design sources is a number read the same way before and after the corpus changes. `evals/ontology/run` gives a reader one case (an engineering decision, or a question about what the publisher most recently said), lets it read the corpus as an agent of this host does, and scores the answer by program: it cites the deciding passage, counted by the overlap of its `path:a-b` with the case's lines, whatever path the file has before or after a move. It also reports the bytes of the corpus an answer read, and whether a case's passage is reachable through the installed route table. Its first user is the Lead who takes the baseline before any file of the corpus moves.

## Requirements

- R1: `python3 -I -B evals/ontology/run` has five verbs: `answers --reader <name> --out <dir> --cases <file> [--samples N] [--case <id> ...] [--corpus <dir>]`, `score --out <dir> --cases <file> [--paths <file>] [--corpus <dir>]`, `gate --baseline <score.json> --candidate <score.json>`, `reach --cases <file> --tables <file> [--tables <file> ...] [--entry <file>] [--instructions <dir>] [--paths <file>]` and `cases --inventory <file> --out <file> [--per-cell N]`. It loads `evals/routing/run` as a module, as `evals/intake/run` does, and copies none of it: the readers, their command lines, the unit of a session, the files of a sample, `run.json`, `gate` and every refusal they share are the routing suite's (`run_sample`, `Sandbox`, `done`, `cost_members`, `report`, `cmd_gate`). The sample's added directory (`a.root`) is `/root/.agents` and the prompt is the case's `situation` alone (`R.PROMPT` is `{situation}`). `answers` refuses `usage` for any reader but `claude-opus` and for `--samples` outside 1 to 9. `--corpus` defaults to `/home/palantirkc/external/reference-docs`, is refused `corpus_invalid` by `answers` when it is no directory, is recorded in `run.json` as `corpus`, and is not given to the reader, which finds the corpus through the installed instructions. `run.json` names the suite by the SHA-256 of the SHA-256 of `evals/ontology/run` followed by the SHA-256 of `evals/routing/run`, so a change of either file, or another `--corpus`, refuses `run_differs`. Tests: `test_answers_starts_claude_opus_with_the_situation_alone`, `test_answers_refuses_every_other_reader`, `test_run_json_names_the_suite_by_both_files_and_the_corpus`, `test_a_changed_routing_file_or_corpus_refuses_run_differs`, `test_a_run_answers_and_scores_with_the_corpus_bytes`, `test_gate_is_the_routing_suites`.
- R2: The one reader is `claude-opus`: the program adds its row to the loaded module's table of readers in memory, built by the module's own builder of a Claude command line (`claude_argv("opus")`, the environment `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, the state `claude_state`). No reader of another model or CLI is built. `evals/routing/run` and `evals/intake/run` are not changed: the SHA-256 of `evals/routing/run` names the routing suite and, with its own file, the intake suite in every recorded baseline. Tests: `test_the_reader_row_is_the_routing_builders_and_the_routing_file_has_none`.
- R3: A case is `{id, kind, cell, situation, deciding, wrong}`: `kind` one of `route`, `trend`, `design`; `cell` a text; `situation` the reader's only message, ending with the fixed sentence `Cite the passage of the corpus that decides this as path:a-b, the file's path and the first and last line of the passage.`; `deciding` a non-empty list of `{source, path, lines: [a, b]}` with 1 ≤ a ≤ b, `source` a key of the file that stays when it moves (its source URL or, where it has none, its path at the corpus commit the cases were cut from) and `path` its path at that commit; `wrong` facts of the routing suite's form `{"fact": [strings]}`, compared after the module's normalisation. A file of another form is refused `cases_invalid`, an id twice `case_id_twice`. `--cases` names the one case file of a call: `evals/ontology/cases.json` (synthetic cases over the fixture corpus `evals/ontology/fixtures/`, for the tests) at its own path, or a host file admitted by its SHA-256 in `evals/ontology/host-cases.sha256` (one line `<sha256>  <short name>`; else `cases_digest_unknown`, a missing file `cases_unreadable`); host and fixture cases never mix in a run or a score, and `score` refuses `case_sets_differ` when the file given is not the one `run.json` names. Tests: `test_a_case_file_is_validated`, `test_a_host_file_is_admitted_by_its_digest_only`, `test_cases_json_holds_the_cut_of_the_fixture_inventory`.
- R4: `score` passes a sample when its answer holds a citation `path:a-b` or `path:a` (a path without white space, quote, bracket or backtick, a colon, a line, optionally a hyphen and a line) whose file is a deciding entry's `source` and whose lines overlap that entry's lines, and no fact of `wrong` holds (the module's `holds`). With `--paths` (a JSON object from a source key to the list of every path the file has had; another form refuses `paths_invalid`) a cited path is resolved only through that map; without it a cited path names the deciding entry whose `path` or `source` it equals or ends with at a `/` boundary (`xfoo.md` does not end with `foo.md`), so a case whose `source` is a URL passes by its `path`. `score.json` holds `suite`, `case_files`, `readers` and `parity` as the routing suite's, and per reader its members (`n`, `passes`, `samples`, `mean`, `majority`, `unanswered`, `tokens_median`, `fn_samples`, `usd`) and, added, `by_kind` and `by_cell` (`passes` and `samples` each, an unanswered sample counted as a failed one) and `corpus_bytes_median`: the median over answered samples of the UTF-8 bytes of the text of the `content` (a string, or the `text` members of its list) of the `tool_result` blocks whose `tool_use_id` is that of a `tool_use` block one of whose input strings (the module's `strings`) holds the corpus path (without its final `/`) at a path boundary: followed by `/`, by the end of the string, or by one character that is not an ASCII letter, digit, `.`, `_` or `-` (with corpus `/c`, `cd /c && sed -n 1,2p a.md` and `cat /c/b` count, `cat /cx/a` and `cat /c.old/a` do not), each block counted once. `score` refuses `run_differs` when a reader's `run.json` names another `--corpus`; it writes only `score.json` and `sheet.md` under `--out`. Tests: `test_a_run_answers_and_scores_with_the_corpus_bytes`, `test_a_citation_passes_when_its_lines_overlap_the_deciding_lines`, `test_a_wrong_fact_fails_a_sample_and_any_deciding_entry_passes`, `test_a_url_source_passes_without_paths`, `test_with_paths_a_cited_path_resolves_only_through_the_map`, `test_score_json_has_the_routing_members_by_kind_by_cell_and_corpus_bytes`, `test_score_writes_only_score_and_sheet_and_refuses_another_corpus`, `test_a_cut_case_of_a_url_source_scores_by_its_path`, `test_corpus_bytes_counts_the_corpus_path_at_a_path_boundary`.
- R5: `reach` runs no reader and writes nothing. For each case, in the order of its id, it prints one JSON line `{case, entry_named, table_named, row}`: `entry_named` whether the file `--entry` (default `runtime-references/OFFICIAL-DOCS.md` under `--instructions`, default `/root/.agents`) is named in `AGENTS.md` of `--instructions`; `table_named` whether each `--tables` file is the entry itself or is named in the entry; `row` the first `<file>:<line>` of a `--tables` file (in the order given) whose line holds a citation, parsed and resolved to a deciding entry as in R4 (with `--paths` through the map), that overlaps the case's deciding lines, else null. A file is named in a text by its path as given, its absolute path, its path under `--instructions`, or its file name. A last line gives the totals `{cases, entry_named, table_named, row, reached}` (`reached` all three); exit 0. An unreadable `AGENTS.md` or entry refuses `instructions_unreadable`, an unreadable table `tables_unreadable`. Tests: `test_reach_reports_the_entry_the_table_and_the_row_of_each_case`, `test_reach_names_and_paths`.
- R6: `cases` reads one JSON array of file objects, the form of the inventory: a file has `path`, `source_kind` (one of `official-doc`, `official-blog`, `official-announcement`, `talk-asr`, `secondary-synthesis`, `catalog`, `work-file`), `publisher_url` (a URL or null) and `passages`; a passage has `lines` [a, b], `concept`, `kind`, `strength` (`normative`, `descriptive` or `promotional`), a non-empty `question` and `quote`; another form refuses `inventory_invalid` and writes nothing. A case's `cell` is its passage's `concept`; a passage whose `concept` begins with `out.` or `other:` gives no case. Kind `route`: up to N a cell (`--per-cell`, default 3, at least 1) among passages of `strength` `normative` in files of `source_kind` `official-doc`; kind `trend`: up to N a cell among passages of `kind` `announcement`, whatever the file. The draw orders a cell's passages by the SHA-256 of `<path>:<a>-<b>` (the draw digest) and keeps the first N; `id` is `<kind>-<the first 12 hex digits of the draw digest>`, so a passage drawn as both kinds gives two ids; the cases are written in the order of their ids, so the same input gives the same bytes. `deciding` is `[{source, path, lines}]`, `source` the file's `publisher_url` or, where it is null, its `path`; `situation` of a `route` case the passage's `question` stripped, an empty line and the fixed sentence of R3; `situation` of a `trend` case the passage's `question` stripped, an empty line, the fixed sentence `Answer from what Palantir most recently said about this in public, in a talk or an announcement, with its event and date, and say whether a page of the publisher's documentation confirms it.`, an empty line and the fixed sentence of R3; `wrong` is `[]`. `--out` is opened and written in place, no temporary file renamed over it, so `--out /dev/stdout` writes to standard output; on success nothing else is printed. `evals/ontology/fixtures/inventory.json` is an invented inventory of this form, with a file whose `publisher_url` is not null. Tests: `test_cases_draws_route_and_trend_cases_per_cell`, `test_cases_refuses_an_inventory_of_another_form`, `test_the_fixture_inventory_has_the_form_of_the_inventory`, `test_cases_writes_its_out_in_place`, `test_a_cut_case_of_a_url_source_scores_by_its_path`, `test_cases_json_holds_the_cut_of_the_fixture_inventory`, `test_a_trend_case_holds_the_trend_sentence_and_a_route_case_does_not`.
- R7: Each requirement of this file names the tests of `ci/test_ontology_suite.py` that hold it, each test cites its requirement as `spec: specs/evals/ontology.md#R<n>`, and one test fails when a requirement names no test of that file or a test is named by none. `evals/README.md` has the section `## The Ontology Engineering suite`, and `map.json` names `ci/test_ontology_suite.py` among the tests of the component `evals`. Tests: `test_every_requirement_names_a_test_of_this_file_and_every_test_cites_one`.

## Interface

`evals/ontology/run` with the verbs of R1; `evals/ontology/cases.json`, `evals/ontology/host-cases.sha256` and the fixture corpus `evals/ontology/fixtures/` (an invented `inventory.json`, invented files under `palantir/` and an invented route table `ROUTES.md`). Every refusal is one line `{"status":"<code>"}` with exit 2. The method and the limits are in `evals/README.md`, "The Ontology Engineering suite"; the tests in `ci/test_ontology_suite.py`.

## Not included

- A model as judge: a statement the publisher's text contradicts is caught only by a `wrong` string or by the Lead reading a failed sample.
- The host cases and their baselines: the Lead cuts them with `cases`, a later unit admits their digest, and the Lead runs them.
- The paths file of a run after a move: the Lead writes it from the corpus's manifest and passes it as `--paths`.
- A reader of another model or CLI, and `--record`: a later unit.
- A place among the suites of the gate of `specs/content/agents-file.md` R9.
