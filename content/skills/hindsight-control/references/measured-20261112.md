**Publisher obligation from the Lead disposition:** an authored original names one to eight `recall_terms`; require each verbatim in at least one extracted fact or its entities before ready. A missing term leaves the record pending with a typed reason; shorten or split the original. Exact-document readback and a token count alone do not prove that the record is findable by its terms. This check adds no extractor or model call and is not a native-server guarantee.

Longest measured extracted fact was 55 tokens and longest actual embedding input 100 tokens; token counts were below byte counts in all six originals, including 988 tokens for 1,147 bytes in an identifier-stress original. Admission bounds belong to the memory design (amendment A21 of thread P10) and are byte bounds, without a tokenizer dependency. The design rationale is that a WordPiece token consumes at least one byte of input, so an embedding input of at most 510 UTF-8 bytes has at most 512 tokens with two special tokens; this inequality has not yet been checked on adversarial strings.
